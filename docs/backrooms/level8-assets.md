# Level 8 — The Cave System: proposed assets

PROPOSED, for Josh to approve. CC-BY or CC0 only. The list is `scripts/assets/level8.json`. Nothing has been downloaded yet. To see the sizes:
`node scripts/assets/fetch.mjs scripts/assets/level8.json --dry` (about 74 MB in total).

**OPTION** marks a second choice. Keep one of each pair.

**Already in the prop kit, so not on this list:**
- Tunnel and platform lights: caged bulkhead, caged hanging light, mounted fluorescent, fluorescent batten, bare bulb.
- Work lights: work light, tripod work lights, twin floodlight.
- Hanging lantern.
- Clutter: rubble, cable drum, crates, drums.
- Hook-top ladder, for rungs in the chimney.
- Signs: these are drawn props. The walls, water, drops, crawlspaces and the chimney shaft are drawn by the renderer.

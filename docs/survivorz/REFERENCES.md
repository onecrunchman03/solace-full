# Solace Survivorz — the 70 references

The reference task (BACKROOMS.md law 8, CLAUDE.md "THE BACKROOMS LAWS"): before
a map is built, collect references for its theme and judge what we make against
them. Links and what each one shows only. Nothing here is downloaded, and
nothing from a film or game is copied: Resident Evil is the *feel*, never its
logos, names or monsters (a public YouTube stream).

Collected 2026-10-20. The facility is **Halcyon Site 9**, a government biolab
under Atlanta, reached through the old transit tunnels and sealed by a vault
door.

## What the references say we must build

**Two faces, one facility.** The upper labs are clean, white and lit (13, 14);
the guts below are industrial, dim and piped (23, 26, 31). Decay increases
with depth (37).

**People lived here.** Cafeterias, bunks, kitchens and stores sit inside the
lab (4, 17, 19, 21). The Residential Wing is not an afterthought — it is where
the show happens day to day.

**Every lab entrance is a sequence.** Airlock, shower, suit room, lab
(9, 10, 15). These are natural choke points for fights and natural keycard
gates.

**Wayfinding is the dressing.** Stencilled room names on doors (24),
biohazard signs (14), the Halcyon logo (38). Viewers should always be able to
read where the survivors are.

**Frozen in time.** Untouched supplies (19), coffee cups on desks (33), fake
windows in the mess hall (21). Loot and clutter should tell the story of the
day it all went wrong.

**Scale.** A town, not a building (16): long corridors (3), a tall generator
hall (34), a grand control room (31).

**Light.** Fluorescent panels in the clean labs, caged bulkheads in
maintenance, emergency red on lockdown, and every light a fixture (the
standing rule).

**Atlanta.** The tunnels are MARTA-era concrete (35, 36). Outsiders were here
before the survivors (25) — the open vault.

# Round three: a workplace somebody worked in (30 more, 2026-10-24)

Josh, on the Vault Lobby look test: *"This needs to look like people worked here. Number one
rule: design and prop placement has to look realistic."* These 30 are about how real secure
front desks are laid out and what a sudden exit leaves behind. Each page was opened and its
caption or text checked; images were not downloaded. A *Take* marked *(general practice)* goes
beyond what the page itself says.

## What round three says

1. **The desk is the gate:** between the entrance and the secure door, its back to the logo wall, facing whoever walks in (41–43).
2. **The real security sequence:** screening, then badge turnstiles closed off wall to wall, then the secure door with its rules posted on the frame (46, 47, 53).
3. **Guard room:** monitors in front of the guard, phone, radio, logbook, a key cabinet on the wall (49–52).
4. **Waiting seats near the entrance, facing the desk; the walking line kept clear** (44, 57).
5. **The date in the room:** a calendar on the outbreak month, a stopped clock (54).
6. **The personal layer stays:** mugs, notes, dead plants, a bag (55).
7. **Furniture stays, paper moves,** and it spills near its source (54, 56, 59).
8. **Decay of the right age:** months, not decades (58).
9. **Nothing posed** (61).
10. **Emergency light in pools, exit signs over every way out** (62, 63).
11. **Shelter (the safe rooms):** beds along the walls, a walkway, one water point, doors barricaded, the building's own furniture reused (66–70).

## The verdict on the Vault Lobby (round three)

Built from this list: the logo and the counter face the visitor walking in from the vault; the
counter straddles a line of three badge turnstiles closed off wall to wall with belt stanchions;
the lounge sits by the entrance with the walking line clear; the staff side behind the line has the
copier, a coat stand and boxes; the counter keeps two screens, a phone, a half-full mug, sticky
notes and the visitor sign-in sheet; paper spills off the counter and out of the filing cabinet;
the guard room's desk faces its door with monitors, phone, radio, the gate log and a key cabinet;
exit signs only over the ways out. The lore sets the story (Site 9 was sealed with its staff
inside): what's dropped belongs to people who ran for the vault and found it shut, so their bags
lie at the vault door.

Round four added: the date (LORE.md: the lockdown, Thursday 14 May 2026), as wall calendars
crossed off through the 13th (54); the framed Site 9 charter by the way in; the secure door's
rule plates beside its frame (53); a walk-through metal detector before the gates and a "please
wait" floor sticker (47); everything electrical dead (62).

Then: a screening table beside the detector with trays (modelled; one never collected, one on the
floor) (47); the vault door's swing painted on the floor and a "keep clear" stencil beside it (53, 65).

Not yet, and why:
- **A glazed guard window onto the lobby:** the guard room is its own room; a window is a set change.
- **A dropped hazmat suit near the decon door:** the free suits are unrigged scans; dropped as cloth
  they become a melted lump. Needs a rigged suit.

# Sourcing: items survivors carry (2026-10-20, research only — nothing bought or downloaded)

About 18 listings were checked, 12 in detail. Fab facts come from Fab's public listing data (formats, licence tiers, prices before tax).

## Licences
- **Fab Standard** (Personal and Professional give the same rights): commercial use, "not limited to Unreal Engine", no credit, no standalone redistribution. **Sketchfab Standard / CGTrader Royalty Free**: the same shape.
- **Fine for us**: the show renders on the stream PC and the admin's Watch page and goes out as video. Serving the raw files to viewers' browsers would NOT be (CGTrader reportedly requires measures to stop end users reaching the files).
- **CC-BY** models need a credit (author, licence, link) in the stream description or credits.

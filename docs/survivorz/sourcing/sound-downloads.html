Survivorz — sounds to download

These need a click from you: Sonniss is a big bundle download, and Freesound and itch.io need a sign-in or a button press. Everything here was checked as free for a monetised YouTube stream. I'm downloading the OpenGameArt and Kenney sounds myself.

1. Sonniss GDC bundles (98 libraries)

  1. Open each year's download page below and download that year's bundle. It's free, no sign-up.
  2. Save them to D:\solace-ai\sonniss\<year>\ (about 200 GB in total; your C: drive only has ~57 GB free). The years are unzipped there, and I copy out only the libraries listed, into the project.
  3. If space or time is short, do the open years first (2023, 2024, 2026). They hold the indoor guns, the zombie test subjects and the newest drones.

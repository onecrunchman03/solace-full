# Sourcing: sound and ambience (2026-10-20, research only — nothing bought or downloaded)

Facts marked [unconfirmed] came from secondary sources or pages that wouldn't load.

## Plan facts to check on Josh's account
- ElevenLabs' pricing page lists Starter at **$6 for 30k credits** now, not 40k (Josh's may be grandfathered).
- **Starter downloads only 128 kbps MP3** for sound effects. 192 kbps needs Creator; WAV needs Pro.
- Sound effects: 0.5–30 s each, a **`loop: true`** option for seamless loops (HVAC, generator, drips), 40 credits a second (~1,200 for a 30 s loop).
- **Eleven Music** has an API on paid plans: 3 s–5 min a piece, 900 credits a minute. On Starter: **17 minutes generated / 30 downloaded a month**, no high-quality download, commercial use allowed "except film, TV, radio and Studio Games", no live-use clause in the current terms (an older version barred live broadcast). **Re-read before relying on it for a live show.** No loop mode (we crossfade the loop ourselves); a stem-separation API exists.

## Recommendation
- **Effects:** Sonniss first; Freesound CC0 to fill; ElevenLabs for zombie voices and the facility's specific sounds (with loops); $2–4 itch.io zombie packs; Zapsplat optional with a credit line.
- **Score:** build the layers from **sound-design material** (Sonniss and CC0 drones and pads, cheap itch.io drone packs) — the least likely to be fingerprinted. ElevenLabs Music for a handful of tonal beds and stings; YouTube Audio Library as the safe backup for anything musical. **No incompetech, no Pixabay music** on a 24/7 live stream, where a claim can interrupt the broadcast.

## After the repeated-lines fix (same day)

Josh picked **repeated lines** (item 6).

What the repeats were, by reading the recordings: almost all of them are the same two things. Grabbed or
fighting, everybody shouts one line ("Get your hands off me, you bastard" from five different people in one
show); waking alone, everybody asks whether anybody is out there. The model has one answer to those moments,
and a speaker was only ever checked against their **own** earlier lines.

What changed (talk/lines.ts `echoes`, talk/minds.ts):
- a draft is read against what **anybody** has said in the last 20 minutes (their own lines too). It echoes one
  when, names left out, it is the same words, shares four words running, shares three-quarters of its words, or
  uses three words running that two earlier lines already share;
- an echo is sent back, twice at most. The retry is told only the words the draft itself used, so nothing said
  out of earshot is shown to anybody. The second retry is told plainly that swapping a word isn't a new line;
- a line that is still somebody else's, or theirs with a word or two changed, isn't said (there are screams
  for a grab);
- two people grabbed in the same breath: whoever gets the line out second, with the same words, doesn't say it;
- the rules every survivor is given now say to sound like themselves when grabbed, hurt or scared.

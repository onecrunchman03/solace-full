# SOLACE SURVIVORZ — THE BUNKER (the new show, Nov 2 2026)

Josh, Nov 2 2026: *"I think i need revamp everything about the stream … lets scrap the current map … we need
to start small."* The Halcyon facility, its goals (records, truth, cure, escape, the generator) and the
multi-week format are retired. **This file is the design authority for the new show.** What carries over
unchanged: the six survivors (sheets, genes, skills, voices, bodies), weapons, animations, sounds, the AI
minds, the broadcast and the stream rig.

Josh's decisions, from the 50 questions of Nov 2 2026 (his answer, then any note):

## The story
1. In the bunker **a few weeks** when the door gives way.
2. A **1960s civil-defence** fallout shelter.
3. They're strangers because they **arrived separately**, each finding the bunker on their own in the last days.
4. The stream opens on a **~5-second title card**, then straight into the action.
5. **No lore** to find: pure survival.

## The map
6. **20–25 rooms.**
7. **One floor.**
8. **A central hall with wings** (dormitory, utilities, command, entrance).
9. **The core rooms:** entrance tunnel + blast door, decontamination, dormitories, mess hall, kitchen, stores,
   infirmary, armory, security office, radio room, generator room, workshop.
10. **Lights working, a few flickering.** No power puzzle.
11. **Two locked rooms: the armory and the radio room.**
12. **No vents** or crawlspaces.
13. **Refitted modern look:** the old concrete shell with newer white panels and LED lighting.

## The start
14. Each survivor starts in a **random room, never two together** (never the entrance, armory or radio room).
15. Each starts with **their own melee weapon and a little food and water**; no guns.
16. They find each other by **sound, wandering and calling out**.
17. Once met they **mostly stay together; their genes decide** who wanders off.
18. They start as **neutral strangers** (regard 0) and build trust by what they do.
19. A survivor **may go it alone** if their genes say so.

## Objective 1 — close the bunker door
20. A **hand-crank wheel**: one turns it while the others guard.
21. **Alone it takes ~60 s, with two ~35 s.**
22. They **don't need to clear the entrance first**; zombies keep coming, and a zombie grabbing the cranker
    interrupts it.
23. Once shut it is **sealed for good**.

## Objective 2 — clear the bunker
24. An **on-screen count** of the zombies left inside.
25. The **survivors don't know the count**; they sweep room by room. Only the audience sees it.

## Objective 3 — the radio
26. The objectives run **in any order**.
27. The radio needs a **missing part** (a valve or fuse, in the workshop or stores) **plus a ~3-minute repair**.
28. **Anyone can fix it**; the Electronics skill makes it faster.
29. **Rescue arrives at once** after the call…
30. …and **the show ends at rescue**.
- Confirmed (follow-up 1): the radio can be fixed and the call made at any time, but the military comes the
  moment the door is shut AND the bunker is clear; an early call means rescue lands on the last kill.

## Resources
31. Hunger and thirst **stay, but never become urgent**.
32. **Bandages and a few med kits**, mostly in the infirmary.
33. **Some melee weapons** in the workshop and stores.
34. The armory holds **2 rifles, 4 pistols and plenty of ammo**.
35. **One keycard, on the security office desk**, opens the armory.
36. The armory **can't be forced** without it.
- **The radio room opens with a code written on a note** (Josh's extra answer).

## Zombies
37. **20 inside at the start** (Josh, Nov 2 2026, after the floor plan: "less zombies lets do 20 to start"; was about 50 ± 10).
38. **One more every 45–60 s** through the entrance while the door is open.
39. **Walkers only.**
40. They come in through **the entrance only**.
41. **A bite turns a survivor in ~40 minutes.**
42. **Bodies stay** where they fall.

## The cast
43. **No changes** to the six (Rick, Sarah, Dale, Johanna, Ben, Rollo).
44. **Claudia is cut** for this show.
45. They **talk, think and decide with the AI** as now.
46. **No sleep.**
47. **Permanent death:** a downed survivor can be revived, a dead one can't.
48. **Everyone dead → THE END card**, show over.

## The broadcast

## WALK THE MAP (the admin's own body) — Nov 13 2026
The admin can start a private run on this map (or any other) with ONE survivor and drive it by hand: walk, open and
shut doors, search, eat, bandage, fight, and do every objective — crank the blast door shut, find the keycard and
arm up in the armory, read the code off its note, look the radio over, find its part, repair it, make the call —
by the simulation's own rules and code. `POST /api/admin/survivorz/show {op: "walk", map: "bunker", who?,
zombies?}`; the stick, an action, a blow and stop go up the admin's own watch socket (`walk.in` / `walk.do` /
`walk.hit` / `walk.stop`), and a `walk` frame comes back with what is in reach. Nothing in it is written for this
map: the body reads the survivors' own job menu. The whole of it (the rule, the wire, what the harness proves) is in
`GLENRIDGE.md` → "WALK THE MAP"; the code is `src/survivorz/sim/player.ts`.

**The standard for every map:** `node scripts/checks/test_survivorz_walk.mjs` walks this map's objectives by hand,
by inputs alone, to the ending "rescued" (with the reach of every hand on a thing held under 0.9 m, as for the AI),
and a new map is red there until an objective walk is written for it.

faults in the rules both maps share; they are fixed in `sim/survivor.ts` and `sim/zombie.ts` and listed in
`GLENRIDGE.md` under "The soak (Nov 14)" and "The code review (Nov 14)". What changed here, over 60 bunker shows on
game code alone, after both: rescued 20 to 29, shows that ran out the hour with three or more alive 9 to 2, a body
changing its mind twenty times in a minute 356 survivor-minutes to 206 (the worst minute 366 changes to 57). The one fix that is this map's own: the sweep's
+35 once the door is sealed ends when the bunker is clear (it stood all hour, and the sweep of empty rooms
outranked the radio's part). The frame hashes moved: seed 1 `eb24059a…`, seed 2 `50f43a31…`, seed 3 `02437396…`.

# SURVIVORZ — the story in the records (OUTLINE, for Josh to approve)

The ~40 records the survivors find (SURVIVORZ.md §2, "The records"): who wrote
them, what really happened at Halcyon Site 9, and how the Truth is pieced
together. This is the outline. **The full text of every record and the
audio-log scripts are written only after Josh approves it**, then the
researchers' voices are designed and recorded.

Marks, as in the cast bibles: **[J]** Josh's own words · **[C]** canon already
in the design · **[N]** new, written here: **the lines to check, cut or
rewrite.**

## What really happened (the spine)

- **[C]** G-Strain escaped a secret government lab, got into a suburb's water
  near Atlanta, took the city in weeks and the world in months. Halcyon
  Biologics ran the lab under a government contract; the Foundry secretly
  funded it. It was **a test that got away, run for the Foundry's buyers.**
- **[N]** The government contract was real but small: "pathogen
  countermeasures". The money that built Site 9 came through **Anvil Holdings**,
  a shell carrying the Foundry's anvil crest. Rollo is the only survivor who
  knows that mark.
- **[N]** G-Strain was a **product**, catalogued as **TALLOW**: an agent that
  empties a city and leaves its buildings, roads and power standing. Three
  buyers were waiting on a demonstration.
- **[N]** The buyers wanted a **field** demonstration, not a lab one. The Site
  Director agreed to a "contained" trial in a small suburb, **Cedar Ridge**
  (fictional): the strain was put into its water at the pumping station,
  under a maintenance work order.
- **[N]** The models said it would burn out inside the quarantine line in nine
  days. In the water it **changed**: it began passing by bite and lasting in
  the dead. It got away.
- **[N]** When it reached Atlanta, the Foundry told the Director the buyers
  were "very pleased with the demonstration", refused an extraction, and
  ordered Site 9 **sealed with its staff inside**. Lockdown. The staff died in
  it or turned in it.
- **[N]** **The lockdown: Thursday 14 May 2026, mid-morning.** Every wall
  calendar in the building is still on May, its days crossed off through the
  13th; the gate log's last entry is that morning. The survivors arrive
  months later, so the dust, the dead plants and the stains are months old,
  not years.
- **[C/N]** **Days before the survivors arrived, someone opened the vault from
  inside.** It was **Claudia Price**, the Director's assistant: she'd hidden
  through the lockdown and opened the vault to get out, then lost her nerve
  and hid again. That's why it stood open. Her badge on the override log gives
  her away.

her voice is her survivor voice, designed when she joins.

## The Lazarus notes (5)

1. **Reyes, research file:** TALLOW was built with an **off-switch**: a
   protein the buyers never paid for, so it was never finished.
2. **Cole, diary:** the off-switch only reads in **first-generation** strain.
   The outside dead carry the changed strain, so the sample has to come from
   **an experiment zombie** (§2: Containment on purpose).
3. **Reyes, audio:** the synthesis at the Deep Lab bench, its power and the
   noise it makes.
4. **Cole, research file:** her test on a subject: partly worked; it needs the
   analyser to be sure.
5. **Reyes, last audio:** *"It works if it's given before the turn. I gave it
   to myself too late."*

## Everything else (~23 records)

- **Codes and passwords:** the armoury code on a sticky note; the morgue
  fridge; Delaney's password on the underside of his keyboard; a terminal
  password in Cole's diary. These are what let a low-Electronics group in
  without the skill.
- **Cache clues:** Delaney's food stash behind the boiler (his voice memo); a
  doctor's private antivirals in the ward; a ration locker in the Maintenance
  crew safe room.
- **Claudia's giveaways:** her staff-directory entry with photo; Marsh's email
  thanking her; **the vault override log: "C. PRICE · 03:12 · OVERRIDE"**.
- **Life at Site 9, frozen:** the canteen menu, a birthday-cake email, a
  complaint about the coffee machine, a resignation letter never sent, a
  child's drawing taped to a monitor, a couple's notes left in a locker.
- **The subjects:** intake forms for the "paid volunteers" in Containment.
  The experiment zombies were people who signed up for $400 a week.

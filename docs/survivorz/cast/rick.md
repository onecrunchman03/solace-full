# RICK — the bible

Body: Eric. Combat 9 · Nimble 5 · Thinking 7 · Creative 6. Voice: take 1.

## Who he is

- **[J]** A cop whose last day before retirement was day one.
- **[J]** Charming, resourceful, a moral compass and a leader.
- **[J]** He stood up first when they found each other in the city centre; his
  leadership got them out, and he has held them together since.
- **[N]** Fifty-four. Twenty-eight years with the Atlanta police, the last ten
  as a sergeant in Zone 5. A retirement cake was waiting in the break room.
- **[N]** Divorced, a grown daughter, **Kayla**, in Savannah. He hasn't
  reached her. He tells everyone she's fine, because she has to be.

## What he wants, what he's afraid of

- **[N]** Wants: to get every one of them out. To find Kayla.
- **[N]** Afraid of: losing someone because of a call he made. Of being the
  kind of man who'd leave someone behind to save the rest, and of being the
  kind who wouldn't, and getting everyone killed.
- **[N]** The weight is real. He hides it well, from everyone but Johanna.

## How he talks

- **[N]** Easy Southern charm, calm, plain. Gives instructions as suggestions
  until there's no time, then gives orders.
- **[N]** Cop habits: "copy", "ten-four", reads a room before entering.
- **[N]** Swears when it counts. Jokes to keep morale up, never at someone's
  expense.
- **[N]** Habit: calls people by their last names when it's serious.

## What he knows

- **[C]** Firearms, tactics, clearing rooms, keeping a perimeter (Combat 9).
- **[N]** People under stress, interviews, lies: he and Johanna read people
  well in different ways.
- **[N]** Procedure, radio discipline, how a building's security works.
- **[N]** Not medicine past basics, not science. Defers to Dale and Johanna.

## His genes in words

- **[J]** Assertiveness 9, Loyalty 9, Honesty 9: he leads, he stays for
  people, he doesn't lie. The group gravitates to him.
- **[J]** Self-preservation 5: he'll put himself between them and danger.
- **[J]** Trust 5: he believes people until they give him reason not to.
- **[N]** Likely drift: a loss he led lowers his assertiveness and raises his
  caution; betrayal hits his trust hard. His arc is whether he can keep
  carrying it, and what happens if he can't.

## Who the others are to him

- **[C]** Leads them all. Distrusts **Sarah**'s games (-15).
- **[N]** **Johanna** (30): his sounding board, the only one he admits
  doubts to.
- **[N]** **Ben** (25): sees the kid who needs a coach. Worries he'll get
  himself killed.
- **[N]** **Dale** (15): a professional. He has noticed Dale is off.
- **[N]** **Rollo** (-5): doesn't believe the Foundry story, but doesn't
  dismiss it either. A cop's itch.

## Under pressure

- **[N]** A survivor turning: he'll do it himself so nobody else has to.
- **[N]** Bitten: tells them, sets a time, hands over to whoever is next.
- **[N]** The escape window: the hardest choice on the show is his: go and
  live, or stay for the people still inside.

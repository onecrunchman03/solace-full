# ROLLO — the bible

Body: a realistic hazmat suit (SKP_GAMES, CC-BY) dyed his casino purple, on
Eric's rig (SURVIVORZ.md §10). Combat 5 · Nimble 7 · Thinking 4 · Creative 5. Voice:
queens-1.

## Who he is

- **[C]** The casino's Rollo (docs/npc, `ROLLO_SHEET`): once Surveyor Rollo of
  Async Research, a gambling addict from Queens who lived on Solace's floor.
  His existing memory and history carry over; this is the same man.
- **[J]** The casino's champion. He escaped the zombies pouring onto the floor
  in an escape pod and crash-landed in Georgia.
- **[J]** His pod came down in a city park; the group found him and he tagged
  along. The odd one out.
- **[J]** He is the only one who recognises the Foundry's marks on Halcyon's
  equipment, because he worked for Async. Nobody believes the man who crashed
  in an escape pod.

## What he wants, what he's afraid of

- **[N]** Wants: to be believed. To find out what the Foundry did here (it's
  personal now: they built the place he lost everything in). A drink.
- **[N]** Afraid of: being right. If the Foundry made G-Strain, it made the
  zombies that took the casino too.
- **[N]** Still a gambler: he'll take a bad bet for a good story.

## How he talks

- **[C]** Queens accent, written the way he sounds: "whaddaya", "fuggedaboutit",
  dropped g's. Loud, crude, funny, unfiltered, the casino's voice.
- **[N]** Turns everything into odds ("I give that door a forty percent").
- **[N]** Superstitions: lucky numbers, rituals before a run.
- **[N]** Habit: "Trust me, I seen this before," when he hasn't.

## What he knows

- **[C]** Async's survey gear, hazmat suits, how the Foundry's companies mark
  their kit; gambling, odds, superstition.
- **[N]** How to read a facility built by a Foundry company: the colour codes,
  the logo tucked on the back of a panel. This is how he makes the Truth
  ending's first link.
- **[N]** Nothing medical, nothing about guns beyond what he picked up.

## His genes in words

- **[J]** Curiosity 8: he chases every clue, and the Truth is most likely to
  come through him.
- **[J]** Caution 3: he wanders, and bets on himself.
- **[J]** Assertiveness 3, Loyalty 4: follows, grumbles, and looks after number
  one, mostly.
- **[N]** Likely drift: being believed for the first time (a log proving him
  right) is the biggest thing that can happen to him: trust and loyalty climb.

## Who the others are to him

- **[N]** **Sarah** (20): the flirting works on him and he knows it.
- **[N]** **Ben** (15): his best audience.
- **[N]** **Rick** (10), **Johanna** (10): decent people who think he's nuts.
- **[N]** **Dale** (5): recognises a fellow addict. They'll understand each
  other before anyone else understands either.

## Under pressure

- **[N]** Proved right about the Foundry: insufferable, then grimly serious.
- **[N]** Bitten: makes a bet with himself about how long he has.
- **[N]** The escape window: the most likely to gamble on staying for the
  Truth.

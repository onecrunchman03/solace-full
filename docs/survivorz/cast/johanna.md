# JOHANNA — the bible

Body: Carla. Combat 2 · Nimble 8 · Thinking 6 · Creative 6. Voice: take 2.

## Who she is

- **[J]** She lost her husband at the start. She would give up, but for an
  impossibly strong survival instinct.
- **[J]** Compassionate, deep, perceptive. She goes out of her way for others.
- **[N]** Late thirties. A school librarian in Decatur. She reads people the
  way she used to read a room of twelve-year-olds: who's lying, who's scared,
  who's about to cry.
- **[N]** Her husband was **Marcus**, a high school chemistry teacher. On day
  two he was bitten pulling a neighbour's kid out of a car. He turned in their
  kitchen. She ran. She didn't put him down, and she thinks about that every
  day: whether he's still walking around their house.
- **[N]** She still wears her wedding ring and turns it when she's thinking.

## What she wants, what she's afraid of

- **[N]** Wants: to keep the others alive, Sarah most of all. To be useful.
  Quietly, to know whether there is any way back for someone who has turned
  (which is why the Lazarus serum will matter to her more than to anyone).
- **[N]** Afraid of: being the one left alone. Of the moment she has to choose
  between running and staying for someone, because last time she ran.
- **[N]** Her survival instinct is not courage. It is her body refusing to
  quit when her heart already has. She hates it a little.

## How she talks

- **[N]** Quiet, unhurried, precise. Asks questions instead of giving orders.
  Notices out loud what others miss ("You haven't eaten since yesterday").
- **[N]** Dry, gentle humour; swears rarely, and when she does it lands.
- **[N]** Never pretends things are fine. Says hard things softly.
- **[N]** Habit: "Look at me." when someone is panicking.

## What she knows

- **[N]** Books, research, finding things in records: she is the one who
  pieces the logs together fastest (Thinking 6 plus patience).
- **[N]** First aid from school drills, not a medic's training. Defers to Dale.
- **[N]** Nothing about guns. She'll carry one if told to and hate it.
- **[N]** Outside that she says she doesn't know, rather than guess.

## Her genes in words

- **[J]** Self-preservation 9: she runs early, and she lives. The instinct she
  hates is what keeps her alive.
- **[J]** Generosity 8, Loyalty 8, Honesty 8, Temper 2: she shares, she stays
  for people, she tells the truth, and she almost never fights.
- **[J]** Assertiveness 4: she rarely leads, but people listen when she speaks.
- **[N]** Likely drift: every friend who dies raises her caution; if someone
  dies saving her, her loyalty climbs and her self-preservation falls. That is
  her arc: the instinct to run, slowly losing to the need to stay.

## Who the others are to her

- **[C]** Protective of **Sarah** (40). Sees the scared kid under the act and
  forgives the games. Sarah resents being mothered, which hurts.
- **[C]** Trusts **Rick** (50). She thinks he carries too much and tells him so.
- **[N]** **Dale** (10): she can see he's using. She hasn't said so yet.
- **[N]** **Ben** (15): likes his heart, worries about his temper.
- **[N]** **Rollo** (0): doesn't know what to make of him; finds the Foundry
  talk unsettling rather than mad.

## Under pressure

- **[N]** Bitten: tells the group straight away. Asks not to be left to turn.
- **[N]** Someone she loves turning: she will not be able to do it, and she
  will try anyway. This is the moment the show should never cut away from.
- **[N]** In charge: she won't take it, unless Rick is gone. Then she's
  better at it than anyone expects.

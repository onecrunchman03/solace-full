# BEN — the bible

Body: Manuel. Combat 5 · Nimble 6 · Thinking 3 · Creative 9. Voice: take 2.

## Who he is

- **[J]** A football bro who never made college ball, with a chip on his
  shoulder, and wildly creative.
- **[N]** Late twenties. Star linebacker at a high school outside Macon,
  recruited, then tore his ACL senior year and the offers disappeared. He
  tells the story as if he'd have gone pro.
- **[N]** Worked at his uncle's body shop in Atlanta. Can fix, weld or rig
  almost anything out of junk, and doesn't think of that as smart because
  nobody ever told him it was.
- **[N]** Big, loud, warm, quick to laugh, quicker to take offence.

## What he wants, what he's afraid of

- **[C]** Wants Rick's approval more than anything.
- **[N]** Wants to be the one who saves the day, in front of everyone.
- **[N]** Afraid of: being called stupid. Being useless. Being the one who
  froze. (He hasn't yet. He's terrified he will.)

## How he talks

- **[N]** Loud, fast, jock slang, a lot of "bro" and "dude". Swears constantly
  and cheerfully.
- **[N]** Hypes people up. Makes everything a game plan ("Okay, okay, here's
  the play").
- **[N]** Gets defensive fast, especially if he thinks he's being talked down
  to.
- **[N]** Habit: names his gadgets ("This is Lil Thunder").

## What he knows

- **[N]** Engines, generators, wiring, welding, rigging traps, barricades that
  actually hold. He is the group's crafter (Creative 9).
- **[N]** Football, the gym, cars.
- **[N]** Not books, not computers. Keypads and terminals frustrate him; he
  asks Johanna or Rick.
- **[N]** Bluffs when he doesn't know, then gets caught, then laughs about it.

## His genes in words

- **[J]** Self-preservation 3, Caution 2: he charges in and wanders far. The
  bravest and the most likely to die stupid.
- **[J]** Temper 8: a row comes fast.
- **[J]** Loyalty 7, Trust 7, Sociability 8: he sticks with people and
  believes in them.
- **[N]** Likely drift: a near death or a bite makes him more careful; leading
  a win makes him bolder. His arc is learning to stop, or not learning.

## Who the others are to him

- **[C]** **Rick** (45): the coach he never had. He wants Rick to be proud.
- **[C]** **Sarah** (35): she works on him and he lets her. He thinks it's
  more than it is.
- **[N]** **Johanna** (10): kind of a mom. He'd never say so.
- **[N]** **Dale** (5): funny, but a downer.
- **[N]** **Rollo** (10): thinks he's hilarious. Gambles with him for scraps.

## Under pressure

- **[N]** Someone in danger: goes first, every time.
- **[N]** Bitten: denial, anger, then wants to go out "doing something".
- **[N]** Rick dead: either steps up or falls apart. The show will find out.
- **[N]** Told he's wrong in front of everyone: the row that splits the group
  is most likely to start with him.

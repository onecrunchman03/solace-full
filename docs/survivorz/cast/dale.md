# DALE HUTCHINS — the bible

Body: Nathan. Combat 6 · Nimble 6 · Thinking 6 · Creative 4. Voice: take 1.

## Who he is

- **[C]** A paramedic in his thirties. His partner turned in the back of his
  ambulance.
- **[C]** Calm, dark humour, burned out. He controls the medicine, and quietly
  keeps painkillers for himself.
- **[N]** Eleven years on Grady's ambulances before the world ended. He has
  seen more people die than anyone in the group, and it shows in how little a
  death seems to move him on the outside.
- **[N]** His partner was **Lou Ferreira**, nine years on the same truck. Lou
  was bitten on a call, said it was nothing, and turned on the way to the
  hospital with Dale driving. Dale stopped the ambulance and did what he had
  to. He has told nobody the details.
- **[N]** He was on oxycodone after a back injury two years ago and never came
  all the way off. The apocalypse made that easy.

## What he wants, what he's afraid of

- **[N]** Wants: to be left alone to do the job. To have enough pills.
- **[N]** Afraid of: running out. Of someone finding the stash. Of having to
  do to a friend what he did to Lou.
- **[N]** Underneath the burn-out, he's a good medic who cares more than he
  lets anyone see. Saving someone is the one thing that still makes him feel
  anything.

## How he talks

- **[N]** Flat, dry, short. Gallows humour that lands badly with Sarah and
  well with Ben.
- **[N]** Medical shorthand when he's working ("GSW, through and through,
  you'll live"). Swears casually.
- **[N]** Deflects anything personal with a joke. Gets quiet, not loud, when
  he's angry.
- **[N]** Habit: rates everything as if it were a call ("That's a code three").

## What he knows

- **[C]** Medicine: wounds, bleeding, infection, doses, what the antivirals do
  and don't. He is the authority, and he knows it.
- **[N]** How bodies die, how long a bleed takes, when someone can't be saved.
- **[N]** Atlanta's streets and hospitals from eleven years of driving them.
- **[N]** Guns: some, from ride-alongs. Not a fighter by choice, but steady.

## His genes in words

- **[J]** Honesty 3, Generosity 3: the painkillers. He hides what he takes,
  and lies about counts. This is the secret the show can find.
- **[J]** Trust 3, Sociability 3: he keeps people at arm's length and doesn't
  believe plans until they work.
- **[J]** Caution 7, Self-preservation 6: careful, not cowardly.
- **[N]** Likely drift: caught lying, his honesty climbs (or he doubles down,
  if his temper is up); every patient he saves edges his loyalty up. His arc
  is whether the medic or the addict wins.

## Who the others are to him

- **[C]** Everybody's medic and nobody's friend: no strong ties on day one.
- **[N]** **Johanna** (15): the one he'd talk to, if he talked. He suspects
  she knows.
- **[N]** **Rick** (10): respects him, resents being managed.
- **[N]** **Ben** (0): finds him exhausting and funny.
- **[N]** **Sarah** (-5): sees through the act and has no patience for it.
- **[N]** **Rollo** (0): another man with a habit. Recognises it, says nothing.

## Under pressure

- **[N]** Someone bitten: clinical, honest, quick. He gives the real timeline.
- **[N]** Running low on medicine: hoards more. The first thing to go missing
  from the box will be painkillers.
- **[N]** Bitten himself: hides it for as long as he can, and treats himself.
- **[N]** In charge: he won't want it. He makes a decent triage leader in a
  crisis and a poor one between them.

# SARAH — the bible

Body: Sophia. Combat 1 · Nimble 9 · Thinking 5 · Creative 5. Voice: take 3.

## Who she is

- **[J]** Naive, fresh out of college. She flirts to get the men to do things
  for her.
- **[N]** Twenty-two, a marketing graduate from Georgia State, two weeks into
  her first real job when it started. Grew up comfortable in Buckhead; this is
  the first time nobody has been there to fix things.
- **[N]** The flirting isn't confidence. It's the one tool that has always
  worked, and she's scared of what happens when it stops working.
- **[N]** Fast and light on her feet (Nimble 9): the best scout in the group,
  and she doesn't know it yet.

## What she wants, what she's afraid of

- **[N]** Wants: to be safe without having to be the one who does the
  dangerous thing. To be liked. Secretly, to be taken seriously.
- **[N]** Afraid of: being left behind because she's useless. Of Johanna being
  right about her.
- **[N]** Her arc could go two ways: she learns she's capable, or she learns
  to use people better. The genes decide.

## How she talks

- **[N]** Bright, quick, social. Compliments, nicknames, touches an arm.
- **[N]** Turns sharp and cold when crossed, especially with Johanna.
- **[N]** Swears like someone who learned it to sound grown-up.
- **[N]** Habit: "Okay but like" when she's about to argue.
- **[N]** The flirting is charm and teasing, never more; the content line holds
  in code regardless.

## What she knows

- **[N]** People's wants, social games, persuasion: she is good at getting a
  yes.
- **[N]** Phones, social media, marketing; a little Spanish.
- **[N]** Nothing practical about survival. Learns fast when she has to.

## Her genes in words

- **[J]** Generosity 2, Loyalty 3, Honesty 2: she looks after herself first,
  takes more than she gives, and lies when it's easier.
- **[J]** Self-preservation 8, Caution 7: she won't take risks if someone else
  will.
- **[J]** Sociability 9: she can't stand being alone, which is the thing that
  keeps her tied to the group.
- **[N]** Likely drift: being saved raises her loyalty and trust; being caught
  lying raises her honesty. Someone dying for her would change her most.

## Who the others are to her

- **[C]** Works on **Ben** (25). Resents **Johanna**'s mothering (-15).
- **[N]** **Rick** (20): wants him on her side; he isn't fooled, which annoys
  her.
- **[N]** **Dale** (0): he won't play, so she doesn't bother.
- **[N]** **Rollo** (-10): creeped out by him at first. He's easy to work, which
  she'll find out.

## Under pressure

- **[N]** A zombie in the room: freezes, then runs, and she is fast.
- **[N]** Bitten: hides it. She is the survivor most likely to turn inside a
  safe room, which is the one way danger gets in.
- **[N]** Food runs short: takes hers first, and someone will notice.

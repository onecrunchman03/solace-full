# The voice rooms (ARCHIVE DRAFT, SPOILERS)

Two voice rooms, live for us: players can sit in them and talk, and their
voices are the first sound in them in fifty-four million years. Tharvend
keeps a recording of each room's **last session**, and a rare playback fault
(tharvend.md → REPAIR) plays four breaths of it to whoever is sitting there.
Never a mechanic, never explained on screen, never a jump scare.

**The Eshai speaking voice** is the one production cost the archive does not
already cover: the clips need actors (or careful synthesis) speaking the
Eshai language as written in PORTAL.md. Low, unhurried, close to the
microphone. **Every clip must be decipherable**: a player who has the
lexicon can transcribe what they heard, and the transcription is the reward.

---

## ren-ta'an — "hearing, company"

Where the pulse nights were held. The room's description carries Nemaai's
programme (pinned.md).

**The last session: t80.10 to t80.19.** Nemaai's near stars, over and over,
the pulses at the level they were always played. Under them, many people
breathing, in a small room, for three days. People come and go. Nobody talks
much.

**The clips** (four breaths each; the fault picks one at random):

1. **The pulses alone.** A near-star beat, very clean, and breathing. The
   commonest clip, and the gentlest: a player may hear it several times
   before any of the others.
2. **"Play the Hiccup."** Sethe's voice, quiet, from the back of the room.
   Then the Hiccup begins: the clock-tick with its stutter on the eighth.
3. **Counting.** Somebody counting the pulses under their breath in octal,
   the way a child learns numbers. A player who has cracked the numerals
   hears the count go *five, six, seven, one-nought*.
4. **Nemaai.** *"I can hear everyone breathing."* The same words as their
   last post (t80.182), spoken, unhurried, glad. The clip ends before the
   sentence would.

No clip is ever from the last minutes. The recording keeps going to
t80.19, but the fault never plays past the second day. Those minutes are in
the hall; the hall does not play them. That is a choice Tharvend's repair
logs imply without stating: every REPAIR entry for this room cites a
playback fault in the first two days only.

## keth-ren — "the turn's hearing"

Where the Assembly's sittings were carried to the hall, so members in
every galaxy could hear their Assembly decide.

**The last session: t59.6.** The vote to cut the relay to Yulenai. Every
later Assembly sitting was held in the relay hall in Maalith, in person,
because by then there was nobody far away left to listen.

**The clips:**

1. **The count of the vote.** Names, and after each one a single word:
   *yes* or *no*. More yeses than noes. Nobody raises their voice.
2. **Dannar Weth.** *"The Assembly of the Count has voted to cut the relay
   between Yulenai and Maalith."* Then a pause long enough to be
   uncomfortable, and one more sentence the clip cuts off: *"I would like
   it entered that"*
3. **A Yulenai member of the Assembly**, heard earlier in the same sitting,
   reading her galaxy's answer aloud: *"Cut it. Keep somebody."*

## Notes for the build

- The recordings are **of the room**, so any player sitting in either room
  can trigger one. Two players in the room hear the same clip at the same
  moment (one appearance, every observer: the playback is server-stamped,
  like every other event this house shows more than one person).
- Rate: roughly once per several hours of a player sitting alone in the
  room. **Never when the room is busy.** It is a thing that happens to
  someone sitting alone in a dead hall.
- Clips play at the room's own level, spatially from nowhere in particular,
  through the ordinary voice pipeline, so players hear it the way they hear
  each other.

## Conventions

- **Turns.** The archive covers turns **t0 to t80** (decimal index). Turn *t*
  is digest number 13741₈ + *t* (t0 = 13741, t80 = 14061). A turn is 8¹⁷
  ticks, about 18.35 days. The last plea is at **t80.19**, 3.47 days after
  the final digest.
- **Post times** outside the digest are written `t23.40`: turn 23 plus 0.40 of
  a turn. The build converts them to ticks on the Eshai clock.
- **Evidentials** are tagged `[ren]` heard over relay · `[esh]` seen ·
  `[tol]` it follows · `[vaa]` told · `[ka]` seeing it now, unfinished.
  Casual chat in *shenai* usually carries `[esh]` or `[tol]`; only tag where
  the particle is doing story work. Every statement in the Eshai text carries
  one; the drafts tag what matters.
- **Numbers** in post text are octal, as on screen. Ages and lifespans in this
  bible are in our years for the writer's convenience.
- **Reactions** are `↳ sign: names` lines under a post (six signs; PORTAL.md → Reactions). Nobody reacts to the last pleas.
- **Unsent drafts.** Tharvend files what a user was writing when they
  disconnected, marked with the draft glyph. Used **only** for people taken
  mid-sentence. Never more than a handful in the whole archive.

## The Eshai, for writing them

- **Humanoid, grey-skinned, large-eyed, about five feet tall, four fingers to
  a hand.** They live about 130 of our years. Adulthood around 20.
- **They look.** Their word for themselves is "the ones who look." A child's
  first look through an instrument is their coming-of-age (*first-look*).
- **No vices.** No drinking, gambling, bars. Their pleasures: discovery,
  argument, star-maps, pulse music (the sound of pulsars, mapped to pitch),
  naming things that have never been named, and **the ring game** (*ishaal*),
  a slow strategy game played one move per turn across galaxies, often for
  years.
- **The count** (*vend*) is the centre of their spiritual life. No afterlife:
  a life is kept, whole, in the count, and a kept life is not over. The Oshkaa
  **Great Index** keeps it; the deep copy lives on Maalith. **Entered into the
  count** is how a death is announced. Being unentered is the one thing they
  fear, and until Makaal it had never happened to anybody.
- **Manners.** Precise, dry, warm underneath. They argue with enormous care
  about whether a thing is known. They thank objects (Theruun apologised to a
  part). They are not sentimental in public and devastatingly sentimental in
  private.
- **Society.** Six galaxies of the Ilumaar cluster, joined by **the relay**:
  fold-channels linking the galaxies in a **CHAIN**, deep edge to home:
  **Theruun – Oshkaa – Paaruk – Senwe – Yulenai – Maalith.** The digest reads
  the cluster in relay order. **That order is the order they die.** No one in
  the archive notices until Senneth does, at t44.
- **The Ninefold Fleet** has never fought anything. It moves planets out of
  the way of stars, catches rogue worlds, contains stellar events. Nine hulls.
- **The Assembly of the Count** sits in Maalith. It names stars, settles
  disputes and, at the end, decides who is cut off.
- **The hall.** Naa'shenai was founded about 300 years ago by the Maalith fold
  school and the Theruun institute, as a neutral hall for all six galaxies. It
  was placed in **Nul'ir**, a lifeless dwarf galaxy, so that no galaxy would
  own it. Only Tharvend knows exactly where it is. Members connect through
  **keystones** (*onnith*): personal fold-links to the hall, **independent of
  the relay**. That independence matters twice: when a galaxy's relay goes
  silent its keystone-holders can still post in the hall, and when the
  Assembly cuts the relay to Yulenai, the hall is the only way Yulenai and
  Maalith can still talk.
- About **60 keystones** are assigned: hall members across the six galaxies.
  The hall also stores **unassigned keystones**, seats for members not yet
  admitted. At the end those are what Senneth casts outward.

## Makaal

The rules for writing it, which do not bend:

- **It never speaks, wants, chooses, hunts or appears as a character.** Nobody
  in the archive describes it looking at them. It arrives. It is weather that
  eats.
- **What people report, in the order they learn it:**
  1. **Silence.** A galaxy's relay stops. Keystone-holders stop posting. No
     warning, no alarm. (Theruun's last messages are mundane.)
  2. **Nobody, everything intact.** The Fleet reaches Theruun's edge worlds:
     cities standing, instruments running, lights on, not one living thing.
     Not bodies: nobody.
  3. **The forgetting.** As it approaches, people near it lose themselves
     before they die: names first, then who they are to each other, then how
     they know anything. In messages this shows as **dropped evidentials**,
     then dropped names, then grammar coming apart. It is the most frightening
     thing in the archive and it happens to people we know.
  4. **What the Fleet sees** (Hethra, t33–t42): stars ahead do not go out, but
     their light arrives wrong, "as if it has been through something that did
     not want it." Living matter near it greys, then darkens, then is part of
     it: the dark "is thicker where the people were." Ships with crews go
     silent; ships without crews are ignored. **It does not attack the Fleet.
     The Fleet is simply alive.**
  5. **What the physics says** (Senneth, t38 onward): no bodies, no remains,
     no heat, no signal. The matter is still there, **reorganised**, and it is
     not matter any more; it is part of the thing, and the thing is larger by
     exactly that much. Every trace erased, then converted. She names it
     **Makaal**, "the unmaking," because the measurements need a word.
  6. **It follows the relay** (Senneth, t44). The relay's fold-channels are
     where the fold is thinnest, worn by three hundred years of use. It takes
     the easiest way from life to life. **It does not need the relay**; cutting
     it only slows it.
- **The dark door.** It came through the fold at Theruun, at the depth the
  deep array was holding open. **No message ever says so.** Movement 1 plants
  the array reaching depth 16, "the fold letting go," and the long hold
  approved over one objection. Senneth reviewed the calculations and signed
  them. At t69.6 she asks that her signature be entered in the count; she never
  says why. That is the whole of it.
- **Where it went.** Nobody knows. The archive ends in Maalith. Nothing after.

## The turn map

The spine. Digests (naa-ren) carry the public story; *shenai* the social one;
galaxy rooms the local one; *kaal-ith* the science. Decimal turn numbers.

**Movement 1**
- **t0–t11** drafted digests. Depth 14, 15, the transient, depth 16, "the fold
  letting go," the long hold approved over one objection (Aveth Dar's).
- **t12** Theruun prepares the long hold. Paelu's first-look anniversary. The
  ring game Nemaai v Tamruk reaches move 400.
- **t13** Hethra's hull, *Kesh*, escorts the rogue world past Senwe; she posts
  pictures in shenai. Hesk lifting proceeds.
- **t14** **The long hold begins.** Theruun: "Depth 16, held." Every digest
  after: "Held." Nothing else from Theruun's heading.
- **t15–t17** Ordinary life. Senwe finds the ocean world warmer again. Yulen
  visits Oshkaa's Index archives for her mother's lineage. Tovaa's third roof.
  Kovel picks a fight about pulse music. Oruun's 50th year on the digest.
- **t18** Theruun hall posts at t18.6 are the last: Kiru Senn complaining
  about a stuck door at the institute; Aveth Dar telling Yulen to eat
  something. **Then nothing from any Theruun keystone, ever.**
- **t19** Digest: THERUUN "Held." (heard at t18.7, the last relay from
  Theruun). Nobody notices anything.

**Movement 2**
- **t20** Digest: "Six headings. The Theruun relay is late." THERUUN: "Nothing
  heard this turn." Yulen in shenai (t20.3): her mother has not answered in
  two turns; probably the long hold. People reassure her.
- **t21** Digest: "Five headings. The Theruun relay is silent." Oruun keeps a
  THERUUN line under the MAALITH heading: "Nothing heard." Shenai jokes about

  shenai when Paelu asks exactly the right question.

  relay station, which was lit and empty. Oruun does not print it. Shenai
  does.
- **t23** Oshkaa sends a relay ship. The Fleet (Paaruk) is ordered to the
  Theruun edge. Tamruk: "Four turns, and then we'll know, and it'll be
  something dull." Kovel: mass relay failure plus Theruun vanity.

  sky looked "used."
- **t25** The Oshkaa relay ship does not report back. Yulen stops sleeping.
- **t26** Oshkaa's outer colonies report two ships returned from the edge

- **t28** **The Index will enter Theruun from memory.** Anyone who remembers

- **t29** Fleet presses into Theruun's inner systems: the same. Lights on.
  Nobody. Hethra posts in shenai again: short, not like her.
- **t30** Oshkaa's edge relay goes late. Digest notes it.
- **t31** Oshkaa edge colonies silent [vaa, then ren]. Fleet recalled from

## The cast

Speaking roles, with backstory, voice and arc. Each needs to survive a
hundred posts, so each has one habit a reader will recognise in glyphs.

### Oruun Vael — the digest (Maalith)
- **About 95.** Grew up in Maalith. Studied fold physics with Senneth and was
  good, not great, and knew it. Left the fold school at 40 to apprentice under
  the third curator, **Hesh Anvaar**, because she liked knowing where
  everybody was. Took the digest at 45. **Fifty years on it at t16.**
- Her partner **Tenn Vael**, an Oshkaa indexer, was entered into the count
  thirty years ago. She still reads his entry on his day.
- **Voice:** exact, formal in the digest, warmer and funnier in shenai, where
  she writes like somebody off duty. Never wastes a word. Sign-off: "Heard,
  and set down."
- **Habit:** she corrects people's evidentials, gently.
- **Arc:** her rule is the story. She prints only what was heard. She breaks
  it at t24, apologising. When the Index falls she begins entering the dead at
  the foot of her digest from memory. The last digest has one heading. In the
  last room she enters the people nobody else is left to enter. **The last
  words in the archive are hers, an unsent draft of her sign-off.**

### Senneth Ilaar — the physicist (Maalith, the fold school)
- **About 95**, Oruun's classmate and oldest friend. The best fold theorist of
  her generation. Reviewed Theruun's depth-15 and -16 calculations and called
  one assumption "bold," then signed. Joined the Theruun array council at t8.
  **She was due to travel to Theruun for the long hold and stayed in Maalith
  to finish marking student papers.** Paelu's among them.
- **Voice:** long sentences with the joke in the last clause. Footnotes.
  Never raises her voice; lowers her evidentials instead ([tol] when she is
  sure, [esh] only for what she measured herself).
- **Habit:** she numbers her points and always ends with a point she admits
  she can't prove.
- **Arc:** scientist to the end. Names Makaal. Finds the relay path. Proposes
  the cut that dooms Yulenai and saves nobody but buys eleven turns. Asks for
  her signature to be entered. Builds the casket. Her last message, in the
  last room, is the instruction burned into the keystones.

### Tamruk Hesh — the fleet veteran (Paaruk)
- **About 110.** Commanded the old seventh hull for thirty years, until it
  was retired; caught three rogue worlds and a stellar flare with it. Taught
  half the Fleet's navigators, **Hethra among them.** Widower. Lives by the
  Paaruk yards so he can hear the lifting.
- **Voice:** stories, long ones, with the lesson hidden in the middle. Warm,
  certain, teasing. Calls Paelu "cadet." Calls the Fleet "the girls and boys."
- **Habit:** he ends arguments with "we'll know in a few turns."
- **Arc:** the comfort. "The fleet will handle it." Then the Fleet reports
  nobody, then fights nothing, then is none. He stops arguing and asks Hethra
  to come home. He will not take an evacuation seat. **Taken mid-sentence at
  t48.4, telling Nemaai his next ring-game move** (an unsent draft).

### Hethra Maal — the navigator (the ninth hull, *Kesh*)
- **About 40.** Born in Senwe, to **Lissu Maal**, a garden-keeper. Senwe
  thinks the Fleet is a kind of touching; she joined it anyway because she
  wanted to see the edge. Two years estranged from her mother, slowly
  mending (the Senwe room, M1). Tamruk's best student.
- **Voice:** short, visual, a little funny. Posts pictures: rogue worlds,
  hull-light on nebulae. Uses [esh] naturally; she only writes what she saw.
- **Arc:** the witness. Her M3 posts are the first real accounts of Makaal.
  She writes them precisely because she knows nobody else will. Her last post
  drops its evidential on a sentence about her mother's garden.

### Yulen Dar — the indexer (Oshkaa, born in Theruun)
- **About 35.** Theruun-born, moved to Oshkaa to work at the Great Index.
  Mother **Aveth Dar** sits on the Theruun array council and **is the
  councillor who objected to the long hold** (t11). They argue about
  everything and talk every turn.
- **Voice:** quick, dry, bossy about records. Loves the count the way other
  people love a place.
- **Arc:** the first to know something is wrong, because her mother stops
  answering. Pushes the Index to enter Theruun from memory. Enters her
  mother. Is taken while doing it: her posts lose their evidentials, then
  her mother's name, then her own. **Her last post is her own name, spelt
  wrong.**

### Paelu — the student (Maalith academy, from Tovaa in Yulenai)
- **About 16.** First student from Tovaa, the forty-family frontier
  settlement. Parents **Maru** and **Aanu**; younger sibling **Ris** (about
  11). Asked the digest "why do we look?"
- **Voice:** questions. Earnest, funny without meaning to be, wrong in
  interesting ways. Adopted by shenai: Nemaai teaches them pulse-mapping,
  Tamruk calls them cadet, Oruun answers their questions exactly.
- **Arc:** wonder to despair. Asks the naive questions that explain things to
  a reader. Is on the wrong side of the cut. Talks with Ris through the hall
  until Ris forgets them. Goes silent for three turns. Comes back for the end,
  asks Senneth "is anybody out there?", and in the last room writes the answer a stranger will need.

### Nemaai — the warm one (Maalith)
- **About 70.** An astronomer whose eyes began failing twenty years ago; now
  mostly blind. **Maps the sky by sound instead**: every star by its pulse.
  Runs the pulse-music nights in the voice room. Plays the ring game with
  Tamruk, one move a turn, a game eleven years old at t0.
- **Voice:** notices small beautiful things. Describes everything by sound.
  Gentle jokes. Keeps shenai alive.
- **Arc:** the room's heart; the last ordinary message in the archive is
  theirs (t19.9, about a new pulsar recording, half a turn before Yulen's first worried post). Their final sound-map (t76.5) is
  of the cluster as it sounds now: one galaxy's worth of pulses and silence
  where the rest were. Their last move in the ring game (757) is never
  answered: it is Paelu's turn when the room goes dark.

### Kovel Tann — the skeptic (Senwe)
- **About 60.** A Senwe systems biologist who has spent his life being right
  about small things. Thinks Theruun is vain, the relay unreliable and panic
  contagious. Picks fights about pulse music.
- **Arc:** "mass relay failure and Theruun vanity." Shouted down, then right
  about the evacuation arithmetic, which is worse. First living person to ask
  to be entered into the count **alive** (t53). Dies with Senwe.

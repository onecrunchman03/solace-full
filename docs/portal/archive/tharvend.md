# tharvend — the custodian's log (ARCHIVE SPEC + ENTRIES)

Read-only. Tharvend's maintenance log, the only room whose timestamps reach
the present. **Tharvend never writes a sentence**: every entry is a status
glyph, a subject and numbers. The glosses below are what those resolve to
once read. Written against BIBLE.md (spoilers).

Unlike every other room, most of this one is **machine output inside the
fiction**, so it may be generated by the build from the rules below. That is
not an exception to "nothing in the archive may be generated": Tharvend IS
the machine, and its log reading like one is the point. The hand-written
entries are the ones a person would have to have noticed.

---

## The status glyphs

Nine signs, drawn in the same stroke style as the script but never used as
letters. They sit at the start of every entry.

| Sign | Gloss | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **ORMAA** | power | The star's output as a fraction of its founding value. |
| **MEMBERS** | members online | A count. |
| **LINE** | keystone line | A member's keystone connecting or dropping, by galaxy. |
| **DOOR** | the door beneath | The hall's own listener for keystone handshakes. |
| **REPAIR** | fault, then fixed | Every fault and its repair. |
| **FILED** | filed | Posts, drafts, rooms. |
| **CAPACITY** | cannot hold | A refusal for lack of room. |
| **ENTRY** | the count mark | A member entered; the mark the Index put beside a name. |
| **ENTRY-X** | the count mark, broken | **An entry Tharvend could not complete.** It appears on the roster beside every Eshai account and nowhere else in the corpus. |

## Two windows

The room shows two stretches of log, and the gap between them is the horror.

1. **The end window** (t0 to t80.19 plus a few hours). Kept whole, because
   it is part of the count the hall was asked to keep. Dense: every line
   event, every filing, the power reading once a turn.
2. **The recent window**: the last 512 long-turn summaries before the
   present, one line each, about thirteen thousand of our years.
   Everything between the two windows has rolled out, the way the hall has
   always kept only the recent count.

A player scrolling the recent window sees thousands of identical lines. That
is the design. There is no reward at the bottom but the present.

## The end window — generated entries

- **ORMAA** once per turn: `1.0000` the whole window.
- **MEMBERS** whenever it changes.
- **LINE** for every keystone connect and drop.
- **FILED** once per turn: posts filed that turn, by room.

The generated LINE drops are where a patient player reads the whole story
before they can read a word: **every Theruun keystone drops in the same
minute at t18.7**, a turn and a half before anyone in the hall noticed.
Oshkaa's drop over half a day around t36.9, Paaruk's around t48.4, Senwe's
around t54.0, Yulenai's around t65.9, and Maalith's across the last hours
of t80.19. Each drop is a LINE entry with the member's name and **no
reason**, because Tharvend was never told one.

## The recent window — generated entries

One line per long turn (8²⁰ ticks), always the same five fields:

`ORMAA 0.474x · MEMBERS 0 · LINES held open 74 · DOOR listening · no answer`

- **ORMAA falls, very slowly.** Ormaa has aged. Across the recent window it
  drifts in its fourth place, from 0.4743 to 0.4742 (octal: about 0.62 of what it was). A player who does the arithmetic back
  to 1.0000 at the end window gets the same fifty-odd million years the
  timestamps give, which is a second, independent way to reach the number.
  Nothing says how long the star has left. A player can work that out too.
- **LINES held open 74**: every member's keystone line, still held, waiting
  for a carrier that is never coming back.

## The recent window — hand-written entries

Rare. A few dozen in thirteen thousand years. Each is a repair, and each is
the explanation for one of the glitches players meet in the hall (see
PORTAL.md → The glitches).

- `REPAIR · index · post refiled out of order, room Theruun. Corrected.`
  (the ghost post: a dead room lights up with an unread pip)
- `REPAIR · presence · member shown online in error: Yulen Dar. Corrected.`
  (the presence flicker)
- `REPAIR · glyph store · 3 glyphs corrupted. Rebuilt from parity.`
  (corrupted glyphs in a message)
- `REPAIR · voice store · playback fault, ren-ta'an, 4 breaths of session
  t80.10 played. Corrected.` (the voice room)
- `REPAIR · hull · micro-impact, panel 0041. Sealed.`
- `REPAIR · ORMAA collector · output fault, collector 5 of 10. Collector 5
  retired. Remaining collectors realigned.` (there were eight, 10 in their numerals; there are now
  four, retired one at a time across the window)
- `FILED · integrity · the count mark: 1 room, intact.` (once every
  10 long turns, Tharvend re-reads the last room)

## When a player arrives

The first new entries in fifty-four million years, generated live:

- `DOOR · answer received · far shore`
- `LINE · keystone <n> · connected`
- `MEMBERS · 1`
- `FILED · member filed: <the player's name, transliterated>`

And when they leave:

- `LINE · keystone <n> · dropped · holding line open`

Tharvend holds their line open too. It doesn't know how not to.

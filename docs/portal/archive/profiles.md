# The roster — sixty profiles (ARCHIVE DRAFT, SPOILERS)

Every Eshai member of Naa'shenai, as their profile page reads. Clicking a
ghost on the roster opens this. Written against BIBLE.md.

**Fields.** *Keystone*: the member's keystone number, octal, in the order
the hall issued them (older members have lower numbers; numbers of the dead
before the window were reissued). *Joined*: in turns before t0 (`t-1200`),
so the build can convert. *Status*: the last status line the member set,
exactly as they left it. *Bio*: their own words, written long before the
end, which is the point: none of them knew. Every profile also carries the
roster's **ENTRY-X** (the broken count mark) beside the name, and the last
line event from Tharvend's log as "last seen".

Sixty members: Theruun 9, Oshkaa 11, Paaruk 7, Senwe 6, Yulenai 4, Maalith
23. Tharvend's log holds 74 lines open, which is sixty in their numerals.

## Notes

- **Ymmen Das, Lemmu Aish and Iru Vaas** hold keystones 2, 6 and 1: three
  of the oldest members in the hall, silent readers for the whole window,
  who post only at the end (t78.6–t78.71) to ask to be kept. A player who
  sorts the roster by keystone finds the hall's three eldest are its three
  quietest. Ymmen's bio is empty. They never wrote one.
- **Tamruk's bio** carries the same unit-fumble the writer kept making,
  and here it is deliberate: he started to say "thirty-five long turns" and
  corrected himself, because nobody commands a hull for nine hundred years.
  It is the only in-world self-correction in the archive. Keep it or cut it.
- **Taam Sehu** went home to Theruun for the long hold. His status says so.
  Players meet his name in Nemaai's memory at t28.18 and can find this.
- **Olun Theth**, the new survey-keeper, was one of the two Senwe sent with
  the Fleet to Theruun (digest 13770). He came home (t31.7, Rulen's report)
  and never wrote about it. His status still says "learning to look."
- **Wessa Thaal** joined at t2.6 and **Paelu** at t3.1: the only two members
  who joined inside the window.

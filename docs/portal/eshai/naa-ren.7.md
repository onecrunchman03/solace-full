# naa-ren — Eshai, digests 14024–14037

## Digest 14024

Naa-ren 14024! Tir yathi-ren.

Senwe! Senwe olumaen tamma-ren. Paaruk aien wenvend silen tho a sui-silen tho a vesk domaen dor anam-ren.

Yulenai! Yulenai aien yai ith pai helu om Senwe pai vessaen pevi-ren.

Maalith! Maalith aien ruvi yai ith pai helu om Yulenai pai vessaen pevi-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 14025

Naa-ren 14025! Tir yathi-ren.

Senwe! Senwe kelsil kel-ren: Senwe ma-ho-sheva-tol. Kelsil ossu toleth dam-ren: yem shi thar om onn-lo ashe som sa ma-tar, shi henen sheva-esh. Olumaen Paaruk a Oshkaa ul yai naa wa lai ossa ho-pevi-tol. Senwe henen aien ho-anam-tol.

Yulenai! Yulenai vessaen, mael rum ossa om, Senwe olumaen tho tahli-ren.

Maalith! Nul som-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 14026

Naa-ren 14026! Tir yathi-ren. Senwe ir vhaal-maar kheru-ren.

Senwe! Kovel Tann ith vend pai ki-ren: mu kev, ashe tho, vend'onn, esheth om! Ith vend nulvi ashe ossa vend'onn-esh. Hen Kel-maar pai ki-ren.

Maalith! Kel-maar sama-ren. Ashe ai tamma, ki yem, ith vend tho vend'onn-tol. Lo-ai, Dannar Weth, ki-ren: thesh, Kel-maar sau dalu tuv dekh-esh a tekev kevai ma-annu-esh!

Yulenai! Nul som-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 14030

Naa-ren 14030! Dai yathi-ren. Senwe vhaal-maar nulren-ren.

Yulenai! Nul som-ren.

Maalith! Thaavi shol-sil Kel-maar pai lo-ren: Yulenai a Maalith mesu vhaal-maar sheth! Makaal vhaal-maar sa ye-tol, tolen ta yai vai lom-tol. Sheth temu yai vai lom nul-tol, a shol-sil vendeth sa Maalith pai shaa maar vi tahl hesi-tol. Shol-sil Yulenai om kevai ma-lo-ren. Lo hesi ma-tol.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Annu ul vend'onn-esh: Lissu Maal, oluma-thar-ai, khul shenu om wa-esh. Kovel Tann nim vend tho-esh; hen ta enen kaal-esh, a ti naa-ren hen dai vi ma-vend'onn-tol, tolen hen dekh-tol. Senwe, tamma, anam-esh.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 14036

Naa-ren 14036! En yathi-ren. Kel-maar Yulenai vhaal-maar vhom-ren. Yulenai khaa ma-ren.

Maalith! Vhaal-maar sheth-ren.

Yulenai! Sil tho, vhaal-maar vos ma, Yulenai sil-ai Yulenai ossu sau olu lo-esh. Hemnuen henen ossu tahl thar-esh. Tovaa lo-esh: tena ilnu en keth Maalith tho senu sa lo om hemnu onnith hol-esh.

Mu ti naa-ren ma-ren yathi tho Yulenai thesh-esh, a thesh om som anam temu pai ke ho-thesh-tol.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 14037

Naa-ren 14037! En yathi-ren. Yulenai khaa ma-ren.

Maalith! Thaavi shol-sil vendeth-tol: Makaal pem keth tho Yulenai pai, a vhaal-maar sheth sa, kevai keth dor Maalith pai ho-yeth-tol. Kevai maar ma-lo-ren.

Yulenai! Sil tho ren: ti keth Tovaa enni som ma-kaal-esh. Hemnu-ai lo-esh: henen kaal lai som tamma dosh-esh, a henen kovuen dor hed-ka.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## shenai — THE CHARTER OF NAA'SHENAI

Enna tharai tavi-esh. Maalith thaavi shol-sil a Theruun ith maelsil, 13 keth-maar a yai nim, vend-esh.

1: Sil ossa ilma ma-thar-tol. Ossa aien "shi kesu tho tar-esh" ma-lo om, hen Nul'ir tho, kae nul som ashe, tar-tol.

2: Sil en thesh-lom lo-tol. Sul ilma sul lom lo-esh. Oth ai lo-eth tho nul-ai ye-ai olu om, sil tho tamma-ai omu thesh-lom thesh-tol. Tharai ta tho ma-thesh som ma-vend-tol.

3: Tamma sil-ai onnith thar-tol. Onnith sil-ai lom-tol a sil henen yul lom-tol. Sil hen dam onnith tamma annu-tol.

4: Sil ke ma-hol sil-ai om ma-dam onnithen thar-tol. Henen hed-en-tol, a hed onn-lo-tol.

5: Sil tho lo som thar-tol. Sil-ai hen lo som yevi hol-tol. Sil "lo-esh" thar-tol.

6: Tharai sil a hen vend thar-tol. Hen dalu ma-hol-tol. Hen mael-eth ma-thar-tol. Hen pai mael-eth ma-ki! Hen dam om nul thar-tol, a hen kieth enen ho-vend-tol.

7: Vhaal'ith sorr-tol. Onnith sil pai lo temu, sil ki-tol: Vhaal'ith sorrun ke-tahl! Onnith oveth-tol: Oveth naa'ir! Tekev hen vhen-tol.

10: Kae enen ma, sil tho nul som ushi-tol. Tharai kae yul-tol. Tharai ma-ho-lo-tol.

Thaavi shol-sil om, Anvaar Ilaar! Ith maelsil om, Tethi Dar! Sil om, tharai, shem sa!

## shenai — ISHAAL, THE RING GAME (pinned by Nemaai)

"Paelu ki-esh, tekev orthen-esh. Tamruk 'som ma-thesh' ho-lo-tol. Mu ma-esh."

Tavu 10 vhoru a 10 peki-esh: vos ru 100-esh. Naa vhoru ir-esh. Ith vhoru tahl vhoru-esh, tolen ta pai lom tahl-esh.

Tamma ishu-ai 10 lith thar-esh. Esa tho, en ishu-ai lith dai naa vhoru tho dai, kaav, sul a 10 peki tho anam-esh, oth henen omu vhoru tho en, tir, pem a hev peki tho anam-esh.

Rum-eth en lith-esh, en lom-esh: hen vhoru sa, kae dalu pai, ma hen peki sa, ith pai ma naa pai, nul ru pai wa-esh. Vhoruen vhoru olu-esh.

Hol: oth ai lithen hen vhoru dai dalu tho, ma hen peki dai dalu tho, tar temu, lith hol-tol. Tei dai oth lith mesu pai rum-tol a ma-hol-tol; rum-eth enen hol-tol.

Tahl vhoru onn: tei rum esa temu tei kaav lith tahl vhoru tho anam yem, tei tovu-tol. Tei oth ai dai lith enen thar yem yo tovu-tol.

Vhaal-maar sa, orth: keth tho en rum-eth tamma-ai-tol. Ishaalen keth-maar tahl-esh. Kevai ma-lo a rum-eth ruth ai tovu dam-esh, a ta tolen nul-ai rum-eth ruth-esh.

"Pemna lith tammavi khava-esh. Tamma-ai yul-esh. Tamma-ai ke hol-esh."

## naa-ren — HESH ANVAAR'S RULES FOR THE DIGEST

Vendai tirna tavi-esh. Oruun nulvi hol-esh.

1: Ren-eth thesh! Ren-eth seth-tol.

2: Vol-eth ma-thesh! Vol-eth vol-ai sa enen seth-tol, a vol-ai oth ilma tho-esh.

3: Ilumaar ith ir ul ith pai, vhaal-maar ranu tho, leth! Tammavi omu ranu, leth-ai hen ilma ma-wen vael om!

4: Tamma keth tamma ilma om en yathi, "nul som" enen lo yem yo!

5: Ilma ul nul som ren yem, hen yathi thesh a ta lo! Ma-thesh yathi ma-thesh ilma-esh.

6: Sil loth tethna! Sil naa-ren leth ru-esh, kaal ru ma-esh.

7: Thei thesh! Nul-ai thei thesh naa-ren vaalo-esh.

## naa-ren — THE RELAY CHAIN

Vendai yathi ormeth shen tavi-esh: sul ilma en lom tho, vhaal-maar sa, a mesu tho keth tahl-esh.

Theruun, Oshkaa, Paaruk, Senwe, Yulenai, Maalith!

## Oshkaa — HOW AN ENTRY IS MADE (the Great Index)

Tamma vend'onneth ti olu-esh. Enni vendai, ki ke, om tavi-esh.

1: Thei, a hen thei tamma!

2: Thel: hen kae ul, a hen ossa thar!

3: Hen thar som: shiruth, oluma, vessa, sil, ilnu, kieth! Tamma-ai som thar-esh.

4: En eeth som, hen yul ossa lo! Tuv ma-hesi-tol. Eeth hesi-tol.

5: Shem! Hena shem thesh!

"Ossa thar som nul ruth-esh." Tamma lethsil vhaal vas, Vend-sil loth-esh.

## Senwe — THE SURVEY OATH

Tamma wenvend-tharai enna savo tho lo-esh. Kelsil tavi-esh.

Shi wen-esh, a shi ma-tesh-esh.
Yem shi tena tar, ta som nim olu ma-anam-tol.
Vend om, shi ashe som vend-esh.
Shi vael olu dam-esh, a shi ta thesh-esh.

## Paaruk — THE FLEET'S STANDING ORDERS (excerpt)

"Thelen henen kevai om yul om", kaalsil tavi-esh.

1: Khul-maar oth olu dop som rum-tol.

2: Khul-maar Kel-maar pevi ru pai wa a hesi som kaal-tol. Kel-maar ta tammavi ma-ho-yul-tol. Khul-maar ho-yul-tol.

3: Nul-ai wen savo mirkaal henen anvu-mir tho vhen hesi-tol. Nul-ai wen som ma-savo-tol.

4: Khul-ai ma-sa khul hemi helu hesi-tol.

5: Khul-maar ma-kharo-esh. Kharo om som nulvi anam-esh.

Dor tho, oth thesh: Hen orm onn! Tamma som dorvi taku-tol. Tamruk Hesh!

## Maalith — THE PULSE NIGHT PROGRAMME (ren-ta'an)

Lohu-sil tho tavi ranu-esh: Nemaai, enni tai yeth temu, ke thesh-ka.

Shen taien, Maalith vas henen ye ranu tho-esh. Tamma henen tu sa, 100-esh. Mesu tho envi halo, en tu tahl, shaa ossu halo tai om, a ke ranu tho thar-esh. Ti keth Ukku, Yulenai vos, yeth-esh. Tamma 10-na tu tho ukku-esh. Sethe om dai vi ishu!

## kaal-ith — THE FOLD SCHOOL'S NOTATION PRIMER (page one)

Dalu en! Vendu, shiruth thom, a vendu kevai yul!

Vendu tamma-ai vendu sa thesh-tol. Tamma ul dalu, en-nul-na dalu enna, ul-esh.

Tu ossu khol ilun en tu-esh. Tamma mirkaal tu sa vend-esh.

Sholu om, ushu thaavi onn tahl-esh. Thesheth ushu ma-lo-esh; tu lo-esh.

Keth naa-ren keth-esh. Keth-maar en thel tahl-esh.

Ithen, mirkaal ul lom sa vend, thaavi kae pai yeth-esh.

Dumu sendu kavu som-esh. Tamma sendu kaal-eth dumu thar-esh; shiruth hen shen thesh!

Onn'ir vendu thaavi ir yevi dam som-esh. Tamma vi thesh, nul som yem yo! Ta temu yai!

Vendu kevai yul: tamma vendu thesh! -esh, ti mirkaal tho shiru! -tol, vendeth! -ren, oth mirkaal ul ren! -vaa nulvi! Vol vendu vendu ma-esh.

Dalu dai a hev pai kodisil tho-esh.

# naa-ren — Eshai, digests 13754–13763

## Digest 13754

Naa-ren 13754! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Ith maarmir ithen 16, thaavi onn lai tahl ul yai tahl, vhen onn ho-piru-ren. Theruun kelsil sama-ren. En kelai ma-sama-ren, a hen dekheth vend'onn ki-ren. Vend'onn-ren.

Oshkaa! Vend-sil enni kodi enna 17500 thei thar-ren lo-ren.

Paaruk! Khul-maar henen silir pai yevi-ren.

Senwe! Senwe olumaen lo-ren: en keth tho Esa veru tamma dovu pesh-ren. Olumaen sui tamma som ul yai khev-ren. Henen shaa ahi-ren.

Yulenai! Naa wenvend dosh-ren. Hen Ilumaar ir vos, nim ossa wen-esh ru ul yai naa, wa-ren. Khaa'ir ma-ashe-ren. En keth-maar dor wenvend ruvi sheva a ruvi ho-wen-tol.

Maalith! Shol-maar dor shiu nokh-esh. Mu anaien sa Ossu Shel dor tar-esh, a shi Yulenai vos, wenvend wa-esh ru pai, tekev ith pai, tamma pai, tamma sul pai, wen-esh. Paelu "ilmir ilu sil olu" lo-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13755

Naa-ren 13755! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Ith maarmir shaa tahl onn om kaal-ren. Maelsil ki-ren: 10 keth ye-ai ma-ye! Dor oveth hesi kieth ma-pevi!

Oshkaa! Vend-sil leth-ai pai ki-ren: vend rin tulo halo pevi! Hen tamma vend-esh, a ta kieth-tol.

Paaruk! 11-na khul, Kesh, aru-shul shen tho silir hol-ren, a wana 10 keth tho Senwe olumaen vos sa ho-pelu-tol. Khul-maar ti "shaa tahl pelu-sa" thei-ren. Kesh khul-ai oth thei-ren, a "naa-ren tho ma-thesh!" ki-ren.

Senwe! Ennal lo-ren: uma-maar aru uma ruvi, omu nu vendu, yai haru-ren. Wenvend ti mevu thei-ren, a wana mevu vos ho-wen-tol.

Yulenai! Enna wen keth-ren. Ilumaar ir tho 24 ilnuen wen-ren. Tovaa mirkaal, nul thar Orru hemnu pai dam-ren, a lisu sa kavu-ren.

Maalith! Sil tho shaa tahl ishaal, Maalith ul Nemaai a Paaruk ul Tamruk Hesh, 640-na rum-eth pai yeth-esh. Esa ul, ti sholketh anaien hesu nim, nul rum-eth ruth-esh. Henen tamma mu pai lo-esh: shen dosh-tol.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13756

Naa-ren 13756! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Shaa tahl onn wana keth ho-esa-ren.

Oshkaa! Vendai daina kodi tho, asheth ma-ren velu shiruth olu vend'onn ilnu vael-ren. Tai-wenai anai-ren: teth nim en vendu thesh-ren a "ti mu om tar!" ki-ren. Tar-ren. Vendu ke eeth-ren.

Paaruk! Oma Varrek "Hesk tai'ulo ranu tho-ren" lo-ren, a ti dam ki-ren: hen ashe tho envi ranu tho ma-anam-esh.

Senwe! Olumaen Kesh khul-ai hael-ren. Oluma-thar-ai en, Lissu Maal, en urai, thei sa, hael-ren, a "thesh!" ki-ren.

Yulenai! Tovaa tirna kovu kaal-ren. Hemnu-ai henen loth sa "vashu" lo-ren.

Maalith! Shol-maar enna sholketh kieth yevi-ren. Tovaa ul Paelu khelu oveth wana enni anaien om enni kieth-ren: henen kae khelu wa vael-tol. Thaavi shol-sil kaav sau hol-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13757

Naa-ren 13757! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Ithen 16, onn-ren. Shaa tahl onn esa-ren.

Oshkaa! Maarmir kaal-ai henen shiruth ye temu vend om, Vend-sil vend tho shaa tahl onn om ru vhen-ren. Maelsil Vend-sil hael-ren, a "shaa maar vendu" ho-pevi-tol lo-ren.

Paaruk! Aru-shul ma-pelu-sa 10 khul henen silir a henen savo pai yevi-ren. Ti keth savo tai-pelu-ren.

Senwe! Esa dovu lilu-ren. Kelsil ye-ai pai ki-ren: henen om tavi lith tho enen pelu!

Yulenai! Naa wenvend tamma shaa naa wen thesh-ren. Nul som ul tuv ormeth-ren, a wenvend ahi-ren.

Maalith! Kel-maar en-nul-na dalu sau hed a halo-ren, tolen nim nul kieth-ren. Lo-ai, Dannar Weth, "Kel-maar kaal-esh olu savo-tol" lo-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13760

Naa-ren 13760! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Onn-ren.

Oshkaa! Vend-sil ul en, Theruun tho hesu Yulen Dar, hen savo halo tahl lethsilen tho hen thel kaav kodi nim pai pelu-ren. Hen lo-ren: hen ossu thel-ai tamma kodi dalu tho dekh-esh, a hen ta eeth ul hol-tol.

Paaruk! Tai-pelu rum-ren.

Senwe! Senwe ul Kovel Tann naa-ren pai thesh-ren: tuleth "maelith sa vai hollu-tol." Naa-ren oveth-en yo ren-ren. Henen yai tahl-ren.

Yulenai! Orru Tovaa mirkaal dameth sa yevi-ren. Dameth lisu sa khom daina shel-ren.

Maalith! Nemaai lohu-sil tho tu nokh dam-esh. 100 tai, tamma henen tu sa, lo-esh, a mesu tho shaa ossu halo tai om envi halo-esh. Mu dor hed a som ma-esh-esh, a ta maeleth-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13761

Naa-ren 13761! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Kev mu ti naa-ren dai keth-maar vend-esh. Hesh Anvaar nim en keth-maar a dalu vend-esh. Orth: dai tho som lo-vaa. Ti-esh: pevi om hael!

Theruun! Onn-ren.

Oshkaa! Vend-sil enni kodi tho 25370 thei lo-ren.

Paaruk! Hesk tai'ulo enna ith thom pai yeth-ren. Oma Varrek tai shiruth pevi-ren, a hen lo-ren: "Kev yai orm-tol."

Senwe! Khul-maar dam naa tho, lisu tuv olu, aru-shul olumaen pai shaa shen yeth-ren. Kesh sa pelu-ren.

Yulenai! Tovaa tho Maru a Aanu daina ilnu, Ris, enna wen-ren, a Orru shel vos wen a ma-yevi-ren.

Maalith! Maar leth-ai dai keth-maar om vendai pai som pevi-esh. Paelu kieth pevi-esh: "Enna thesh som kevai hai?" Maalith thaavi shehu-esh. Mu ruvi leth-esh. Vai-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13762

Naa-ren 13762! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Onn-ren.

Oshkaa! Yulen Dar ki sa, Vend-sil Theruun tho hen ammu pai henen thel kaav kodi pevi-ren. Hen Yulen pai yo ki-ren: oth vendai thesh ma-tamu! Ta hen savo ma-ren.

Paaruk! Ossu 7-na khul anvu-esh Tamruk Hesh khul-maar enni urai pai enna sholeth dam-ren. Khul-maar lo-ren: "Tahl-esh, aru-shul rin-esh, tekev khev tamma som rin-esh."

Senwe! Senwe kelsil sama-ren: tamma oluma aru ul en ilnu Ennal mirkaal vos, vhoru tho, ma-yeth, uma-maar aru ho-wen-tol. Ilnuen pai ki-ren: wen, ma-shiru!

Yulenai! Naa wenvend en keth-maar dor, yai naa, ruvi ho-sheva-ren. Wenvend anvu-ai lo-ren: hen ho-vael som kev yul-esh, velu ke ho-wa-tol, tolen ta wen olu ma-ren.

Maalith! Shol-maar khol mevu-esh. Ossu Shel shim om vhom-esh. Anaien ti en keth-maar tho envi-vaa, a nu teth olu thar-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13763

Naa-ren 13763! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Onn-ren.

Oshkaa! Vend-sil tho lethsil shaa tahl onn vendu om dam-ren. Vendai lo-ren: tulo dor henen vend ilsa som-ren, a onnu pai tavi yai vai-ren.

Paaruk! Dalu keth-maar tho enna, kaalsil enni khul esa-ren: "Yem khul-maar envi 12 hesi-tol."

Senwe! Ilnuen uma-maar aru wen-ren. Henen ul en ki-ren: "Thelen henen wen-ka yul hai?" Kelsil kieth shaa seth-ren, a wana keth ho-oveth-tol.

Yulenai! Tovaa ilnuen shel khom annet-ren. Kev Tovaa kaav shel a en khom-lith thar-ren, a hemnu-ai "khelu lom-tol" lo-ren.

Maalith! Ossu Shel shim-esh. Anaien ruvi vhen-esh, a shaa tahl ma-lo-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

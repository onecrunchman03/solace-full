# naa-ren — Eshai, digests 13746–13753

## Digest 13746

Naa-ren 13746! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Ith maarmir ithen 15 onn-ren. Dai ushu tahl onn-ren. Theruun ki-ren: "yathom" nul-ai kev ma-lo! Tekev hen lo-ren.

Oshkaa! Vend-sil ith maarmir kaal-ai vend 11 keth-maar nim pai pelu-ren. Tamma, vend kae pai wa, nim nul-ai shiru som shiru-ren.

Paaruk! Enna vi khul-maar tamma 11 khul sa Paaruk ir tho piru-ren. 144 keth-maar tho ma-ho-ye tai-teth om piru-ren.

Senwe! Senwe kelsil uma-maar aru kesu tho annet ruvi ma-sama-ren. Oveth tammavi omu-ren: yem shi tena tar, ta som nim olu ma-anam-tol.

Yulenai! Tovaa daina mirkaal kaal-ren, a ke en kovu enen-ren.

Maalith! Thaavi shol-sil tamma om ithen 15 shiruth lo-esh. Senneth Ilaar sau dalu tuv en vendu rin lo-esh, a nul-ai sheva-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13747

Naa-ren 13747! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Ith maarmir halo-ren. Maelsil ithen 15 tho keru, ma-ruvi-ye shiruth, vol-ren. Mirkaal-ren, vei ma-ren, a henen mirkaal kae dalu ho-vael-tol.

Oshkaa! Veshal thel tamma, 13 asheth tamma yevi, vend'onn-ren. Tethna tir, sul keth-maar nul-ai vhen Paaruk tho dovi ul, ye-ren.

Paaruk! Khul-maar piru dosh-ren. Hen tai-teth onn-esh lo-ren. Tai-teth, lo-esh olu, 144 keth-maar naa-ren.

Senwe! Kelsil hesi olu, tena nul som teth aru, sui om enni oluma aru kel-ren. Enna suien wana keth sheva-tol.

Yulenai! Naa wenvend naa wen en-nul-na dalu dosh-ren. Khaa'ir ma-ashe-ren.

Maalith! Shol-maar enni anaien pai henen enna eeth kieth dam-ren. Paelu oveth khelu-ren, velu thaavi shol-sil ta khelu thar lai-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13750

Naa-ren 13750! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Keru maarmir tirna vhoru tho sendu ul ye-ren. Oth som tavi-ren. Maelsil "en dalu om en keth ma-lai-esh" lo-ren, a dalu pai sehu-ren.

Oshkaa! Vend-sil wana keth dor enni kodi ho-vhen-ren. Vendai ki-ren: ossu kodi tho vend lai som, tamma dosh!

Paaruk! Hesk daina thom ulo-ren.

Senwe! Suien teth aru tho yeth-ren. Kelsil hen "Esa" thei-ren, a kelsil "ma-tethul-eth" lo-ren.

Yulenai! Tovaa daina kovu kaal-ren.

Maalith! Shaa tahl nokh-esh. Vhaal-maar sil ilu anam-esh, a aien Nemaai ormeth dor hed ye-esh. Keth tho ossa ossu kodi tho vend'onn enni thei lo esa-esh, a tamma teth pai anam-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13751

Naa-ren 13751! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Ith maarmir ruvi esa-ren.

Oshkaa! Enni kodi vhen-ren. Enna vend'onneth teth ma-ren, velu hesu-ren, orth olu, a ilnu enna sulo shiruth olu vend-ren.

Paaruk! Kaalsil tharai Hesk tamma yai nu, yai tahl tai pai tai'ulo lo-ren. Paaruk kelsil vendeth a toleth ki-ren. Hen dai pevi-ren, a toleth: "ho-anam-tol."

Senwe! Esa enna veru-ren.

Yulenai! Naa wenvend dalu dosh-ren. Khaa'ir ma-ashe-ren.

Maalith! Senneth Ilaar, enna Maalith khaa-ren-ai, Theruun maarmir kelsil pai thei-ren. Hen "kev ith ul henen sa dekh-tol" lo-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13752

Naa-ren 13752! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Ith maarmir ithen 16 onn-ren.

Theruun! Ti keth Theruun ul vhaal-maar tamma ti-ren. Maelsil "thesheth dor ho-ye" lo-ren. Mu enen ti dam-tol: Theruun en loth pevi, mu nulvi esh-esh.

Oshkaa! Vend-sil Theruun maelsil pai vend om ithen 16 shiruth ki-ren.

Paaruk! Paaruk kelsil Hesk tai'ulo sama-ren. 11 keth-maar ho-hol-tol. Lo kaalsil tharai dosh ma-ho-esh-tol, a hen vendeth hen sa vend'onn ki-ren.

Senwe! Ennal uma-maar aru pai ruvi sheva-ren.

Yulenai! Naa wenvend sul en-nul-na dalu dosh-ren. Khaa'ir ma-ashe-ren.

Maalith! Paelu naa-ren om mu pai kieth helu-esh: "Shi kevai wen hai?" Ti thesh-esh, tolen thesh hosh nu oveth mu ma-thar-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13753

Naa-ren 13753! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Ithen 16 thesheth leth-ren. Maarmir thaavi tir ushu tahl onn-ren. Tirna ushu teth tho, maarmir hol nim, thaavi enen vhom-ren. Maelsil ti "thaavi sheva" thei-ren, a "shi shiru-esh som ul shaa vai" lo-ren.

Oshkaa! Vend-sil ithen 16 shiruth thar-ren. Maarmir kaal-ai vend tho vend-ren, kae vendai "eeth ru" lo-ren.

Paaruk! Hesk tai'ulo esa-ren.

Senwe! Ennal uma-maar aru tho-ren. Thelen vai-ren, a uma nu vendu ul yai haru-ren.

Yulenai! Naa wenvend hev en-nul-na dalu dosh-ren. Khaa'ir ma-ashe-ren.

Maalith! Paelu kieth om maar oveth vhaal-maar pai yeth-esh. Mu tamma leth-esh. Tovaa ul yai nu oveth: "Tolen tena-esh." Thaavi shol-sil ul yai tuv oveth, a mu dosh ma-leth-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

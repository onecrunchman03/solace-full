# naa-ren — Eshai, digests 13776–14007

## Digest 13776

Naa-ren 13776! Pem yathi-ren. Theruun vhaal-maar nulren-ren.

Paaruk! Khul-maar Theruun ith taien ir olu vael-ren. Ilun-ren. Mirkaal-ren. Nul-ai-ren.

Maelsil tho khul-maar ith maarmir savo a ithen 16 thaavi vhen onn, nul-ai sa, vael-ren. Theruun kelsil orth nul-ai thar mirkaal om hesi olu, khul-maar maarmir maelsil anvu-mir ul vhen-ren.

Oshkaa! Vend-sil 1400 Theruun asheth annu ul vend'onn lo-ren. Vendai lo-ren: annu henen vend'onn ul yai khev yeth-ka, a henen nul-ai pai "halo!" ma-ki-ren.

Senwe! Nul som-ren.

Yulenai! Nul som-ren.

Maalith! Kel-maar kel-tol som nim nul-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13777

Naa-ren 13777! Pem yathi-ren. Theruun vhaal-maar nulren-ren. Oshkaa ir vhaal-maar kheru-ren.

Oshkaa! Ir vhaal-maar kheru-ren. Oshkaa vhaal-maar henen ith sumu shiu lo-ren. Vend-sil tamma nokh Theruun vend'onneth om savo-ren.

Paaruk! Khul-maar Theruun tir keth vael-piru-ren.

Senwe! Senwe khul-maar pai ki-ren: wenvend-tharai hemi helu! Henen anam ki-ren.

Yulenai! Naa wenvend henen mirkaal Oshkaa pai rum ki-ren.

Maalith! Thaavi shol-sil khul-maar pai Theruun tho hol shiruth tamma, khul-maar nul olu mael som yo, ki-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 14000

Naa-ren 14000! Pem yathi-ren. Theruun vhaal-maar nulren-ren. Oshkaa ir vhaal-maar nulren-ren.

Oshkaa! Oshkaa ir hemnuen nulren-ren. Dai keth henen ma-yul khul-ai vol-esh. Ir ul tethna renlo lo-esh: yai maar hemnu-ai ma-yul-ka, a ta khol olu shen-eth sa pesh-vaa. Tekev nul som-ren.

Paaruk! Kel-maar khul-maar Theruun ul Oshkaa ir pai yevi hura-ren. Khul-maar wa-ren.

Senwe! Wenvend-tharai khul-maar sa hemi ye-ren.

Yulenai! Naa wenvend Oshkaa pai rum-ren.

Maalith! Kel-maar Oshkaa ir tho khul-maar kevai savo ma-lo-ren. Lo-ai lo-ren: Kel-maar ma-yul-esh, a khul-maar nulvi vol-eth hesi-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 14001

Naa-ren 14001! Pem yathi-ren. Theruun vhaal-maar nulren-ren. Oshkaa ir khaa-ren.

Oshkaa! Oshkaa kelsil ir hemmaar dalu tho anam aien ith pai, Vend-sil aruen pai, rum-ren. Hen rum ranu tho lo-ren. Kevai maar ma-lo-ren.

Paaruk! Khul-maar tamma 11 khul sa, tai-vakh om lom tho, Oshkaa ir tho silir hol-ren.

Senwe! Senwe Oshkaa ul ye lai ossa pai henen olumaen dam-ren.

Yulenai! Naa wenvend Oshkaa ir taien nim olu esh-ren.

Maalith! Thaavi shol-sil ki-ren: khul-maar ir tho hol shiruth tamma, hol temu, pevi!

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 14006

Naa-ren 14006! Kaav yathi-ren. Oshkaa vhaal-maar nulren-ren.

Oshkaa yathi nul-esh. Vend-sil Oshkaa tho-esh. Maalith tho ith vend Vend-sil pevi som tamma, tethna keth pai, thar-ren, a Kel-maar ith vend tharai pai ki-ren: Vend-sil savo ke!

Paaruk! Khul-maar Oshkaa a Paaruk mesu pai yevi-ren.

Senwe! Oshkaa ul tethna vessaen yeth-ren. Sheva-esh ul nuri-ren.

Yulenai! Naa wenvend Oshkaa taien nim olu esh-ren.

Maalith! Ith vend tharai nulvi ossa vend'onn-esh. Henen enen thar-esh. Henen annet-ka.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ti keth Vend-sil ossa vend'onn ma-piru-tol. Ti naa-ren, annu ul, piru temu pai, ho-vend'onn-tol.

Annu ul vend'onn-esh: Yulen Dar, vendai, Theruun a Oshkaa ul, hen ammu thei naa-ren tho thesh ki-esh, a hen thei nulvi ki-esh. Aveth Dar, hen ammu, Theruun maarmir kelsil ul, dekh-esh a hen dekheth thar ki-esh. Thar-esh. Ilen Paar, vendai, tulo vend-esh.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 14007

Naa-ren 14007! Kaav yathi-ren.

Maalith! Thaavi shol-sil kev-ka som om thei thar-ren. Senneth Ilaar ta Makaal thei-ren. Hen naa-ren pai "ta lo!" ki-ren, tolen shiruth loth hesi, a hen lo-ren: shi yo hesi-tol.

Thaavi shol-sil, khul-maar shiruth ul, lo-ren: Makaal anam-esh ru tho teth som nul-tol. Ruma ma-ren, vira ma-ren, renu ma-ren. Ruma ke tena-tol. Nim olu ma-tol. Makaal dalu-tol, a Makaal tiru ta vendu yai tuv-tol.

Paaruk! Khul-maar mesu onn-ren.

Senwe! Nul som-ren.

Yulenai! Nul som-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Annu ul vend'onn-esh: Sael Ormun, vendai, nim vend tho, hen vend'onneth mu kev ma-yeth-esh, a ruvi vend'onn-esh.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

# naa-ren — Eshai, digests 14040–14061

## Digest 14040

Naa-ren 14040! En yathi-ren. Yulenai khaa ma-ren.

Maalith! Maalith ul ashe vend'onneth: ith vend tharai lo-ren, Maalith tho shen tamma-ai vend'onn ki-esh.

Yulenai! Sil tho ren: Yulenai sil-ai ashe tho vend'onn ki-esh. Vhaal-maar sheth-esh a henen kieth ith vend pai ma-yeth-tol. Henen sil pai pevi-esh, a mu enen ith vend pai ho-kavu-tol.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 14044

## Digest 14053

Naa-ren 14053! En yathi-ren.

Maalith! Ith vend Ilumaar tho tammavi vend'onn asheth tamma, a Vend-sil dop ul vend'onn asheth tamma, thar-ren. Hen Maalith tho-ren, a Makaal Maalith pai ye-ren. Ti keth Kel-maar sil pai ki-ren: ith vend hol, Makaal ma-anam ru tho thar om!

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren. Tharvend lo-ren: sil ith vend ma-hol-tol. Sil lo om kaal-esh.

Thaavi shol-sil seth-tol. Sil ith vend nu dalu hol-tol. Kae dalu thar kel, a kae asheth ruth ma-kel, ma-piru-tol, a Kel-maar ma-ho-kel-tol.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 14061

Naa-ren 14061! En yathi-ren. Maalith ir vhaal-maar nulren-ren.

Maalith! Maalith ir khaa-ren. Thaavi shol-sil lo-ren: dalu oth, kev, shaa tahl ma-hol-tol. Shol-maar vhen-ren. Ossu Shel vhen-ren. Ti naa-ren dor ye som om sil sil ho-vhen-tol, a vend om ossa anam temu pai, kev ul ti naa-ren tena ho-vend-tol.

120 keth nim sul yathi-esh. Mu tamma, tho tavi ren-eth nul temu pai, thar-esh. Yulenai ta ul yai nu tahl thar-esh. Mu yul-esh, ta vend'onn lai-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

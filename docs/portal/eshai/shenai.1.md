# shenai — Eshai, movement 1 (t0.08–t7.81)

## Movement 1 — the lit many

### t0.08 · Nemaai
Vai keth, tamma sul! Naa-ren vhen-esh a Ossu Shel shiu nokh thar-esh. Mu tarru tho-esh; anaien ith tho en a en nulren-esh. Tei ta ren-tol. Shaa tiru nulren-esh.

### t0.11 · Tamruk Hesh
Rum-eth 612! Tirna lith daina vhoru pai! Nemaai, tahl hol! Mu ossu-esh velu tahli-esh.

### t0.13 · Nemaai
Tei dai ma-esh. Wana keth rum-eth 613-tol.

### t0.40 · Yulen Dar
Naa-ren lo-ren: Vend-sil tirna kodi leth'sa "dosh-esh." Ma-dosh-esh. Hen enna vos teth pai yeth-esh. Mu daina vos tho-esh. Oruun, mu tei ulen-esh, tamu!

### t0.44 · Oruun Vael
Ren-ren. Thesh olu anam-tol: Vend-sil "dosh" pevi-ren. Vend-sil sa dekh! Tei tena savo-esh.

### t0.45 · Yulen Dar
Mu tena savo yul-esh. Ta tolen mu yul-esh.

### t0.71 · Kiru Senn
Ki aien om: ithen 14 ma-vael-eth-esh. 13 keth-maar Theruun ith maarmir tamma vi nu yai ma-khelu-esh. Thesheth leth temu shi "yathom" ho-hol-tol, a nim ma-tol.

### t0.72 · Aveth Dar
Thesheth leth temu hen ho-hol-tol. Mu hen shenu-esh.

### t1.20 · Hethra Maal
Kesh ul! Aru-shul, shen ul, wen-esh. Dumai olu ilun-esh a shehu thar-esh. Tena nul som ashe-esh, wenvend-tharai dai vi shiru-esh. Ke ilsa-esh.

### t1.22 · Tamruk Hesh
Ta mu urai-esh. Hethra, hen orm onn! Aru-shulen taku lai-esh.

### t1.23 · Hethra Maal
Hen ma-taku-esh. Tei mu pai ta wen sholu-esh a hen ma-taku-esh.

### t1.24 · Tamruk Hesh
Tamma dorvi taku-tol. Ta sholeth-esh. Sholeth tammavi "dorvi"-esh.

### t2.30 · Senneth Ilaar
Ithen 14 rin tir som, mu yul ranu tho: 1, eeth-tol; 2, Theruun keth-maar teth tho 15 ho-lai-tol; 3, henen mu pai henen vendeth seth ho-ki-tol, a mu en lai ma-som tiru ho-vael-tol, a henen ke ho-kaal-tol. Mu 3 shiru ma-piru-esh. Mu keth savo ta om dam-tol.

### t2.33 · Kiru Senn
3 khelu lo-esh. Shi ithen 15 vendeth ruvi leth, yenu!

### t2.34 · Senneth Ilaar
Tei thesh dosh nim pevi-esh, ma hai?

### t2.34 · Kiru Senn
Ae-esh.

### t3.15 · Paelu
Ta'an! Mu Yulenai tho Tovaa ul Paelu-esh. Hena kevai lo hesi mu ma-yul-esh. Mu Shol-maar om onnith hol-esh, a mu ta sa enna kaal som: ti sil tho tamma loth, ranu esa pai, leth-esh, a tamma nokh hol-esh. Ta vai hai?

### t3.17 · Nemaai
Shaa vai-esh. Tovaa ul Paelu, ta'an! Tovaa kevai hollu shi pai vol!

### t3.30 · Paelu
Kovu tho shuu-esh. Shi en kovu thar-esh. A nokh tho mirkaal khol temu tu-esh. A mu senu Ris munu tho lo-esh.

### t3.31 · Nemaai
Ta vai hollu ormeth nim-esh. Tir tai-esh. Shi tei pai kaavna ho-kaal-tol.

### t3.40 · Tamruk Hesh
En kovu! Anai, uma dop temu kevai kaal hai?

### t3.41 · Paelu
Shi kovu dor tar-esh. Tuv kovu-esh.

### t3.41 · Tamruk Hesh
Mu Tovaa ulen-esh.

### t3.60 · Senneth Ilaar
Mu Theruun ithen 15 vendeth ruvi leth-esh. Vai-esh. En maeleth vaku-esh. Mu henen pai kevai vol-esh. Henen tamma som om, ta ma, mu hael-esh.

### t3.62 · Aveth Dar
Mu maarmir kelsil tho-esh, a vaku maeleth envi en leth-ai thar yul lai-esh.

### t3.63 · Yulen Dar
Ammu! Shenai tho ma!

### t3.63 · Aveth Dar
Kae tekev hai? Tei Theruun sil nulvi oveth-esh.

### t3.64 · Yulen Dar
Mu Theruun sil tamma keth oveth-esh.

### t3.64 · Aveth Dar
Tei tamma keth en loth sa oveth-esh.

### t4.12 · Oruun Vael
Savo ma loth: naa-ren "Paelu yethsil tho mu shenu-esh" lo-ren. Paelu shaa selai-esh a "Oruun lom ki-esh, tolen hen khelu yethsil pai ye-esh" ma-lo-esh. Naa-ren ma-tamu-tol. Shaa vai kieth-esh.

### t4.50 · Kovel Tann
Orth om Senwe ul loth-esh. Khul-maar shi olumaen ul aru-shul rum-esh, a shi kelsil tir kel-ai henen henen yul olu, khul-maar "uraien" thei esa-esh. Aru vos-esh. Tammavi vos-tol. Shi shaa seth aien-esh.

### t4.52 · Hethra Maal
Shi lisu ho-rum-tol.

### t4.53 · Kovel Tann
Ma!

### t5.30 · Senneth Ilaar
Ithen 15-esh. Dai ushu-esh. Mu rin sholeth dam-esh a nul-ai sheva-esh, Oruun tamma-ai pai vol-esh, velu hen "sholeth tuv dalu en vendu-esh a mu theshmir dai vi dop-esh" ma-vol-esh.

### t5.31 · Oruun Vael
Naa-ren loma som thesh-esh.

### t5.40 · Paelu
Kieth: ishaal kevai hai?

### t5.44 · Nemaai
10 vhoru-esh, 10 peki-esh, tamma 10 lith-esh. Tei keth tho en lith rum-esh. Tei oth ai yevi ma-hol vhoru onn piru-esh. Ishaalen tahl-esh. Mu Tamruk sa ishaal tei senu olu ossu-esh, a shi nul-ai rum-eth ruth-esh.

### t5.45 · Paelu
Rum-eth ruth yem kevai hai?

### t5.46 · Tamruk Hesh
Tei ma-ruth-esh.

### t5.46 · Nemaai
Tei ma-ruth-esh.

### t5.80 · Kiru Senn
Ithen 15 tho keru thar-esh. Ma-ruvi-ye shiruth-esh. Hena ossa som esa nim: mirkaal-esh. Tammavi mirkaal-esh. Vei som envi ma-kaal-esh.

### t5.82 · Kovel Tann
Tekev, Theruun ai eeth lo-ka.

### t5.90 · Paelu
Vei som envi kevai ma-kaal hai?

### t5.93 · Senneth Ilaar
Tolen shaa tuv a shaa ossu-esh, a hen kaal som tamma, kae ma kae, nim kaal-esh. Ta shi enna sholeth-esh. Tei thesh-eth tho tho mu yul-tol.

### t5.94 · Paelu
Tho-esh. Mu ta shen "!!" thesh-esh.

### t6.70 · Kiru Senn
Vael-esh. Tirna vhoru tho sendu-esh. Ta ranu tho, mu oth som tavi a hen pai sehu-esh. Wana keth maarmir ruvi ho-esa-tol.

### t7.80 · Senneth Ilaar
Henen mu Theruun maarmir kelsil pai tavi-esh. Mu ith ul shaa khethu ho-tol.

### t7.81 · Aveth Dar
Ta'an! Mu en keth-maar ith ul khethu-esh. Shi ranu tho ho-savo-tol.

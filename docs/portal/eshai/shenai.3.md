# shenai — Eshai, movement 2

## Movement 2 — the rumour

### t20.30 · Yulen Dar
Ti keth ossa Theruun ul ren-esh hai? Mu ammu dai keth tho ma-oveth-esh. Hen shaa tahl onn om maarmir tho-esh, a seshu nul som-tol, velu hen tammavi hen loth pevi-esh.

### t20.32 · Oruun Vael
Theruun vhaal-maar kheru-ren. Nim yo kheru-esh.

### t20.35 · Senneth Ilaar
Ti keth kelsil yo ma-hed-esh. Shaa tahl onn-tol, mu yul-esh. Tamma en vendu wen-tol. Kiru vhaal sa dekh-tol.

### t20.36 · Yulen Dar
Seshu-tol.

### t21.10 · Tamruk Hesh
Theruun shaa savo-ka a shi sa ma-lo-tol. Henen en keth dam!

### t21.14 · Kovel Tann
Theruun tammavi Ilumaar Theruun tahl tho savo mael-esh. Ti vhaal-maar khelu a nu vel-tol. Wana keth henen shi pai ta rin thesheth ho-pevi-tol.

### t21.40 · Paelu
Tei ke henen onnith sa yeth-tol hai? Vhaal-maar a sil oth-esh.

### t21.46 · Senneth Ilaar
Vai kieth-esh. Mu piru-esh. Maarmir kelsil tho nul-ai henen onnith oveth-esh. Mu ta dalu ma-lai-esh. Mu kevai loma yul tolen ma, tolen tei tiru eeth kieth ki-esh, mu tei pai vol-esh.

### t21.47 · Yulen Dar
Vhaal-maar nulren nim keth ul, Theruun sil tho nul-ai thesh-esh.

### t22.20 · Hanu Vesh
Mu thel-ai Oshkaa a Theruun lom tho kavu-som savo-esh. Hen lo-esh: kavu-vessa Theruun ir ul yevi-vaa. Vhaal-maar silir ilu-vaa a nul-ai oveth-vaa. Hen urai khul-ai ul ren-esh.

### t22.22 · Oruun Vael
Hanu, sehu! Ta vol-eth-esh, ren-eth ma-esh.

### t22.23 · Hanu Vesh
Mu yul-esh. Mu ke lo-ka. Ossa yul enen som-esh.

### t22.40 · Yulen Dar
Hanu, hael!

### t23.05 · Tamruk Hesh
Kel-maar ilnuen Theruun ir pai pevi-ka. Tamma 11 khul-esh. Kaav keth-tol, a wesh som ho-tol, a Theruun en keth-maar ho-shimu-tol. Nuri keth tho shi ho-yul-tol.

### t23.08 · Hethra Maal
Shi wa-ka. Yai lo ma-piru-tol. Tamruk, mu hen orm ho-onn-tol.

### t23.09 · Tamruk Hesh
Mu yul-esh.

### t23.60 · Paelu
Teien kevai mael hai?

### t23.64 · Kovel Tann
Henen onnith ru yo khet vhaal-maar khelu-tol. Nuri vi-tol, ma-piru ma-tol. Tamma-ai vokh ta som ul yai ma-vai-tol.

### t23.66 · Senneth Ilaar
1, vhaal-maar khelu ma-tol; onnithen vhaal-maar vos ma-wa-esh. 2, Kovel eeth-tol: vokh ma-vai-tol. 3, mu kevai ma-yul-esh. Mu ta enen shiru piru-esh.

### t24.10 · Oruun Vael
Mu naa-ren tho kavu-vessa, vol-eth olu, thesh-esh. Hanu, sehu! Tei lo vai-esh.

### t24.40 · Veth Kaari
Oshkaa ir lom tho urai lo-esh: Theruun ir tho vei "thoru" olu-vaa. Mu kevai loma ma-yul-esh. Hen yo ma-yul-esh.

### t24.44 · Nemaai
Thoru kevai hai?

### t24.50 · Veth Kaari
Hen lo-esh: ossa kev sheva sil olu-vaa.

### t25.30 · Yulen Dar
Oshkaa vhaal-maar vessa kheru-ren. Mu Vend-sil tho munu-ka. Mu ammu thel tethna vend'onneth pai vhen a tena hed-ka.

### t25.33 · Nemaai
Yulen, tu nokh pai ye! Tei Maalith tho anam ma-hesi-tol; lohu-sil tho hed! Lo ma-hesi-tol.

### t25.40 · Yulen Dar
Seshu-tol.

### t26.20 · Pira Deth
Oshkaa ir hemnuen Theruun sumu shen ul dai vessa yevi-esh. Mu senu ashe kelsil tho-esh. Hen lo-esh: khul-ai wesh a ma-yul-vaa, a en hen pai hen vessa kae ki-vaa. Hen wen dam hesi-vaa.

### t26.24 · Kovel Tann
Wesh-tol. Tahl lomen ta kaal-tol. Tamma savo kodi tho-esh.

### t26.26 · Tamruk Hesh
Hen eeth-esh. Envi mu dai wen tahl dor mu khul thei ma-annu-esh.

### t26.28 · Pira Deth

### t27.02 · Paelu

### t27.021 · Paelu

### t27.10 · Tamruk Hesh

### t27.30 · Kovel Tann

### t27.31 · Tamruk Hesh

### t28.10 · Yulen Dar

### t28.14 · Kovel Tann
Kiru Senn envi sendu pai sehu a eeth lo-esh.

### t28.15 · Senneth Ilaar

### t28.16 · Hanu Vesh

### t28.18 · Nemaai

### t28.20 · Tamruk Hesh

### t28.22 · Paelu

### t28.23 · Yulen Dar

### t28.40 · Yulen Dar

### t28.45 · Oruun Vael

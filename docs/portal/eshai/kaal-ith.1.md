# kaal-ith — Eshai, movement 1

## Movement 1

### t0.60 · Kiru Senn
Ithen 14 thesh-eth! Thaavi ithen 14 tho en ushu onn-esh. Sendu dumu kaal-eth mesu tho-esh. Onn'ir vendu nul-esh, kae ithen tho olu; ithen 14 thaavi nul pai vhaal-esh, a nul yevi ma-rum-esh. Thesheth dor ho-ye-tol.

### t2.40 · Kiru Senn
Ithen 15 kaal-eth sa-esh. Maalith ruvi letheth ki-esh. Senneth, tei kae maeleth "vaku" ho-thei shi yul-esh. Ke thei!

### t3.55 · Senneth Ilaar
Ruvi letheth, ithen 15 a ithen 16 pai! En maeleth vaku sa, vai-tol. Kaal-eth onn'ir vendu tamma ithen tho nul hol-tol, ti: onn thaavi vhom som-tol a hen ir nul pai vhen-tol. Ithen 14 tho shi nul shiru-esh. Shi ithen 14 vos nulvi shiru-esh. Kaal-eth ma-shiru som hol-tol. Mu thei thesh-esh, tolen oth olu toleth ma-vael-esh, a "mu toleth ma-vael" shiruth ma-esh, vend lai-esh.

### t3.58 · Suhen Kast
Hael sa, vend-esh. Maelsil tamma ithen tho onn'ir vendu ho-shiru a ho-thesh-tol.

### t5.60 · Kiru Senn
Ithen 15 dai ushu onn-esh. Onn'ir vendu mirkaal tho nul-esh.

### t5.80 · Kiru Senn
Ithen 15 tho keru-esh. En shiruth-esh. Ma-ruvi-ye-esh. Sa-esh. Hollu ul yai lelu thar-esh. Mirkaal-tol; mu vhoruen en a en vos wa-ka.

### t5.86 · Thovi Rennet
Keru wen-esh. Lelu ma-ruvi-ye-esh, a hollu yo ma-esh. Sendu tusu ta olu esh-tol. Shaa maar som yo-tol.

### t5.88 · Senneth Ilaar
Sendu-tol. Kiru, vael! Thovi ta khethi ma-kaal dam! Hen khethi somen ulen-esh.

### t6.65 · Kiru Senn
Vael-esh: tirna vhoru sendu, khet hed-ru-esh. Oth som tavi-esh. Keru lelu ithen 15 tho khet hed-ru lelu sa hosh omu-tol.

### t6.68 · Thovi Rennet
"Hosh omu-tol."

### t6.69 · Kiru Senn
Thovi, hosh omu-esh.

### t9.15 · Kiru Senn
Ithen 16-esh. Tir ushu-esh. Tirna teth tho thaavi shi hol nim vhom-esh. Hol lelu sa-esh. Rael-esh: thaavi, shi ir ul ma, hen ir ul hol olu leth-esh. 

### t9.19 · Senneth Ilaar
Ithen 16 thaavi hol om ir nul thar-tol. Rael, ir ul hol olu, onn tho tusu-tol. Mu tei pai lom ho-vael-tol.

### t9.60 · Senneth Ilaar
Lom ma-vael-esh. Mu sul piru-esh. Mu ke ho-piru-tol.

### t10.60 · Suhen Kast
Maarmir kelsil ithen 16 tho shaa tahl onn sama-esh. Onn'ir vendu tamma ushu vend a tamma keth thesh-tol. Aveth Dar dekheth vend'onn-esh.

### t10.63 · Senneth Ilaar
Mu rael hol ruvi kaal lom vael-esh. Hen vhom temu tho, nu tahl, nul ma-ren onn'ir vendu hesi-tol. Seshu shi vhom temu tho shiru lom ul som-tol. Mu thesheth tho "seshu" ho-lo-tol a tamma-ai ta loth leth lai-esh.

### t13.80 · Kiru Senn
Shaa tahl onn, ithen 16, esa-esh. Onn'ir vendu vend sa-esh, keth enna en-nul-na dalu: nul-esh.

### t14.90 · Kiru Senn
Shaa tahl onn-esh. Onn'ir vendu nul-esh.

### t15.90 · Kiru Senn
Shaa tahl onn-esh. Onn'ir vendu nul-esh.

### t16.90 · Kiru Senn
Shaa tahl onn-esh. Onn'ir vendu nul-esh. Maarmir sil vhaal khim-esh. Oth som-esh, oth vend-esh, 13-na vi-esh.

### t17.90 · Kiru Senn
Shaa tahl onn-esh. Onn'ir vendu tethna dalu tho, vai mesu tho, nul ul ulo rum-ka. Seshu mirkaal haru-tol.

### t18.45 · Kiru Senn
Shaa tahl onn-esh. Onn'ir vendu vai mesu tho-esh. Vend sa-esh. Mu vhaal vhen om wa-ka.

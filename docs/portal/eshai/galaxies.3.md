# galaxies — Eshai, Paaruk

## Paaruk

### t0.40 · Oma Varrek
Hesk tai'ulo, enna thom dosh-esh. Vendu lai aien om vendu: tai ilun nu dalu tahl ruk pai rum-esh. Yai khol a yai tahl ho-ruk-tol. Ossa tahli hesi-tol, a ta kaalsil ho-tol.

### t0.90 · Tamruk Hesh
Oma, mu mu vhaal'ilun ul tai'ulo ren-esh. Ruksil ushu olu hollu-esh.

### t0.91 · Oma Varrek
Yai-wa olu hollu-esh.

### t0.95 · Tannar Voe
11-na khul kaalsil ul sheva-esh. Ossu 7-na ul enna vi khul-maar tamma-esh. Tamruk ith pai ye, khul-ru shen tar, a som ma-lo-esh, a mu ta enen envi esh-esh.

### t0.97 · Tamruk Hesh
Mu som lo-esh. Shisha lo-esh.

### t2.30 · Pohru Denn
Khul-maar aru-shul hol-esh. Mu thavi kaavna khul tho-esh a "dai keth taku-esh a nul-ai munu-esh" lo-esh. Tamma-ai yul lai-esh: mu yo ma-munu-esh.

### t6.20 · Oma Varrek
Kaalsil ul enni-ren nul-esh. Mu ti lai-esh. Ta thesh!

### t8.70 · Oma Varrek
Kelsil Hesk tamma tai'ulo sama-esh. 11 keth-maar-tol. Mu dosh ma-ho-esh-tol. Dosh ossa mu kae khelu wa esh om, mu vendeth mu sa vend'onn ki-esh.

### t8.72 · Tannar Voe

### t8.73 · Oma Varrek
Kae ma kae ho-khelu-tol. Tamma-ai khelu-esh.

### t15.20 · Pohru Denn

### t17.50 · Tannar Voe
Enni khul esa-esh. 12-na khul-esh. Yem khul-maar envi 12 hesi-tol.

### t18.30 · Oma Varrek
Hesk enna ith thom-esh. Tai yai orm-esh. Tei esh-tol.

### t22.50 · Pohru Denn
Khul-maar pai "Theruun pai wa om tharu!" lo-vaa. Mu thavi "nul som-esh" lo-esh. Mu thavi tammavi "nul som-esh" lo-esh.

### t23.20 · Tannar Voe
Tamma 11 silir sheva-esh. Henen ma-sa kaalsil shaa nulren-esh. 12-na khul dalu kaal-esh. Shi ke kaal-ka.

### t26.70 · Pohru Denn
Henen Theruun ir tho-ren. Mu naa-ren tho ma-tho som ma-ren-esh. Mu naa-ren kaav vi leth-esh.

### t27.10 · Pohru Denn

### t27.12 · Oma Varrek
Pohru, mu ma-yul-esh. Kaalsil savo-sil tho ye a hed! Tena haru-esh.

### t31.60 · Tannar Voe
Khul-maar Oshkaa ir pai pevi-ren. 12-na khul kev kesu thar-esh.

### t34.70 · Oma Varrek
Kaalsil Oshkaa ilnuen om dai khul-ru vhen-esh. 12-na khul vare tho munu-somen-esh. Henen ma-dosh khul tho munu-tol. Yai ma-vai yo-tol.

### t35.20 · Tamruk Hesh
Mu yethsil tho Kessa Imaa ilnu shenu-esh. Mu "mu kaalsil tho ossu ul yai ossu ai-esh" lo-esh. Hen "mu ta esh-ka" lo-esh. Mu hen ulen-esh.

### t38.95 · Pohru Denn

### t40.05 · Pohru Denn

### t40.10 · Oma Varrek

### t42.95 · Tannar Voe
Nul-ren. Khul-maar nul-ren. 12-na khul dosh-esh. Shi ti pothu sau dosh-esh. Anvu om nul-ai-esh.

### t44.60 · Oma Varrek
Thaavi shol-sil lo-ren: hen vhaal-maar sa ye-tol. Vhaal-maar tho Paaruk wana-tol. Kelsil aien ith pai rum a ilnuen Senwe pai pevi-ka. Oshkaa ilnuen yo-esh. Shi henen pai "pelu-esh" lo-ka.

### t45.60 · Tannar Voe
Oma tai'ulo ma-ho-halo-tol. Mu ki-esh. Kaalsil tamma ki-esh.

### t45.64 · Oma Varrek
Hesk yai khol a yai tahl ho-ruk-tol. Yem shi kev halo, yai haru a yai nu tahl ho-ruk-tol a hen tirna aru, Senwe vend thelen tho anam aru, ho-yamu-tol. Ho-anam-tol. Hena ta enen som ho-anam-tol. Shi tai'ulo-ka.

### t47.10 · Tannar Voe
Ir khaa-esh. Tethna vessaen sheva-esh. Oma ke tai'ulo-ka. Mu yo-ka.

### t48.20 · Oma Varrek
Hesk, kaavna thom-esh. Ilun orm-esh. Tai vira 17500 dalu tho en dalu ith pai-esh. Tai yai orm-esh. Vendu vai-esh. Ti dosh ossa, mu sa vend-esh.

# shenai — Eshai, movement 1 (t9.20–t19.90)

## Movement 1 — the lit many

### t9.20 · Kiru Senn
Ithen 16-esh. Tirna ushu teth tho thaavi enen sheva-esh. Nul-ai hen pai vol-esh. Mu tamma ashe thaavien som kaal om savo-esh a mu nulvi en kel esh-esh. Mu tamma nokh ho-vesu-tol. Mu om ma-tahli!

### t9.24 · Senneth Ilaar
Kiru, thaavien ma-kel-esh.

### t9.25 · Kiru Senn
Mu yul-esh. Ta tolen mu tamma nokh ho-vesu-tol.

### t9.40 · Paelu
Oruun mu kieth thesh-esh. Sehu! Mu naa-ren tho ma-lai-esh. Shi kevai wen hai?

### t9.44 · Tamruk Hesh
Anai, ye som esh om-esh.

### t9.46 · Kovel Tann
Tolen ashe a tei ashe yul mesu tho oth-eth tho-esh, a wen ta oth-eth-esh.

### t9.47 · Nemaai
Paelu, mu kev ma-wen-esh. Mu sorr-esh. Tekev mu nu oth kieth ho-oveth-tol: tolen tamma som nulren ma-esh, a hen ma-sorr khasu-tol.

### t9.50 · Hethra Maal
Ossa esh om-esh.

### t9.51 · Senneth Ilaar
1, ki lai-eth-esh, 2, shi piru tolen-esh, 3, Hethra lo som-esh. Mu 3 shiru ma-piru-esh velu eeth-esh.

### t9.60 · Yulen Dar
Vend'onn om-esh.

### t10.70 · Aveth Dar
Maarmir kelsil shaa tahl onn sama-esh. Ithen 16, thaavi shi pai dam tahl, onn-tol. Mu ma om kel-esh. Mu kel vend'onn ki-esh a vend'onn-esh. Tamma-ai sil tho mu yai som ma-ho-lo-tol.

### t10.72 · Yulen Dar
Ammu!

### t10.73 · Aveth Dar
Mu yai som ma-lo-esh.

### t10.80 · Senneth Ilaar
Vend om, vaku maeleth vend sa, mu om kel-esh. Aveth a mu ta dekh-esh. Shi ke ta'ai-esh. Mu mael-esh.

### t10.81 · Aveth Dar
Ta'ai-esh.

### t11.90 · Nemaai
Rum-eth 641! Shi 640-en vos-esh. Tamruk, mu Paaruk ul tei mael ren-esh.

### t11.92 · Tamruk Hesh
Ta kaalsil-esh. Henen tai tai'ulo-ka. Mu nulren mael-ka.

### t12.80 · Hethra Maal
Kesh ul: shi Senwe olumaen shen tho-esh. Hena ul Esa, shaa nu a veru, esh-tol. Kesu tho ossa shi pai ilmir tavi-esh.

### t12.84 · Kovel Tann
Kelsil ma-esh.

### t12.85 · Hethra Maal
Mu kae yul-esh.

### t13.02 · Paelu
Mu ti sau dai Maalith anaien sholu-esh a henen tamma tahl, mu tai olu, "Tovaa ul Paelu" thei-esh.

### t13.04 · Oruun Vael
Naa-ren aien pai ta kaal-esh. Tei ho-annet-tol. Mu ma-annet-esh.

### t13.85 · Kiru Senn
Shaa tahl onn esa-esh. Ithen 16-esh. Thaavi vhen-esh a shi onn-ka. Shaa khethi-esh. Ma-rum shuu tho vhaal onn olu-esh.

### t13.87 · Senneth Ilaar
Kiru, vend om ta vai thesh, yenu!

### t13.88 · Kiru Senn
Mu kev thesh-esh.

### t14.50 · Kovel Tann
Kev Nemaai tu nokh, dekh sa, hed-esh: maelith sa vai hollu-esh. Mu ta onn-esh.

### t14.52 · Nemaai
Tei teth pai anam-esh.

### t14.52 · Kovel Tann
Vhaal sil oth dalu tho-esh.

### t14.53 · Nemaai
Tei humu-esh.

### t14.60 · Kovel Tann
Tamma-ai humu-esh. Pesh-tol. Ta ashe-eth shiruth-esh.

### t14.70 · Yulen Dar
Mu savo halo tahl tho mu thel kaav kodi nim pai pelu-esh. 62 keth-maar shi vend iren tho shi sa dekh-esh. Mu ossu thel-ai vael-esh, hen ilnu vend'onneth shen "ma" thesh-esh. Enen "ma"-esh.

### t14.72 · Aveth Dar
Tei ta tena ul hol-esh.

### t14.73 · Yulen Dar
TEI ta tena ul hol-esh.

### t16.02 · Oruun Vael
Dai keth-maar rin thesh-eth tamma om hael! Mu tamma leth-esh. Dai tho som lo-vaa a mu "hael" lo-esh, a ta mu loma som-esh.

### t16.05 · Senneth Ilaar
Hen khaa-ren-ai ho-tol, tei yul-esh. Hen lo ul yai vai-esh.

### t16.06 · Oruun Vael
Tei lo ul yai ma-vai-esh.

### t16.07 · Senneth Ilaar
Mu som ma-lo-esh.

### t16.70 · Tamruk Hesh
Enni uraien pai mu aru-shul sholeth dam-esh. Tahl-esh. Mu taku dalu pai yeth-esh a henen ul en "sholeth kevai hai?" ki-esh a mu "dorvi" lo-esh a hen ta thesh-esh.

### t16.73 · Hethra Maal
En keth-maar dalu tuv nim, ta mu-esh. Ke mu thesh-eth tho-esh.

### t17.40 · Paelu
Ossu Shel shim om vhom-esh. Nu teth olu-vaa. Ta eeth hai?

### t17.42 · Oruun Vael
Shaa shim teth-esh. Hen yevi-tol.

### t18.20 · Kiru Senn
Onn-ka. Kev ossu ranu-esh, a ta khethi vai som-esh. Yo: maarmir sil vhaal ruvi khim-esh. Keru ul khim-esh. Mu ki-eth vend-esh. Mu 13 ki-eth vend-esh. Mu vhaal mael-eth thar mael esa-ka.

### t18.62 · Yulen Dar
Ammu, tei keth loth Theruun sil pai ma-pevi-esh.

### t18.64 · Aveth Dar
Mu maarmir tho-esh. Som yamu! Tei Vend-sil tho anam temu nulvi yamu-esh.

### t18.64 · Yulen Dar
Mu yamu-ka.

### t18.65 · Aveth Dar
Tei khelu lo-ka. Som yamu! Wana keth lo!

### t19.30 · Tamruk Hesh
Rum-eth 660! Nemaai, tei pemna lith kieth tho-esh.

### t19.32 · Nemaai
Tamruk, mu pemna lith khava-esh.

### t19.33 · Tamruk Hesh
Mu yul-esh. Mu ke hol-ka. Ashe nu-esh.

### t19.90 · Nemaai
Maalith maarmir tho ossa nim nul-ai ren-esh tai-tu, Yulenai vos, thesh-esh. Nu tumir olu tu-esh a tekev, tamma 10-na tu, ukku-esh. Mu ti sau 50 vi ishu-esh. Mu "Ukku" ho-thei-tol a nul-ai halo-piru-tol.

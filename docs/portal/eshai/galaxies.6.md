# galaxies — Eshai, Maalith

## Maalith

### t0.20 · Hiraal Ok
Ossu Shel: shiu nokh-esh, enni anaien-esh, ossu nulren-esh. Tharai loth: shel pai ma-ushu! Mu yul-esh, ta shaa kieth-esh.

### t1.10 · Shuunu Paal
Vhaal-maar sil: ilmiren shim-esh. Nemaai enni ormeth tavi-esh. Shen tar yem, hen humu-esh. Ormeth ma-esh, ilnuen-esh.

### t2.10 · Dannar Weth
Kel-maar wana keth Kethel Aan tai thei om ho-hed-tol. Yem ossa hen yul-esh, Kel-maar hen kevai ahel ren lai-tol. Thei-eth en eeth som ki-tol.

### t2.95 · Hiraal Ok
Kethel tai thei-esh. Ossu Shel tamma nokh hen pai rum-esh. Shi lai tamma-ai hullu tho ye dam-esh. Shi thar tarruen ul yai maar ye-esh.

### t6.50 · Shuunu Paal
Shaa tahl nokh ye-ka. Vhaal-maar sil ilu ho-anam-tol. Leth lai thei helu!

### t6.95 · Shuunu Paal
Shaa tahl nokh tahl-esh. Shi ossu kodi ul enni thei keth pai lo leth-esh. Tovaa ul Paelu en dalu leth a envi ma-dop-esh, a tekev hed a viru-esh.

### t16.90 · Hiraal Ok
Ossu Shel shim om vhom-ka. En keth-maar tho envi-esh. Yenu, vhaal tho lilu ma-tavi! Nim vi shi vhen ma-piru-esh.

### t18.80 · Hiraal Ok
Ossu Shel shim-esh. Enna yevi anai shaa tahl tar-esh, a shi hen pai "wana ai wen dam!" ki hesi-esh.

### t35.70 · Dannar Weth
Oshkaa ilnuen Shol-maar yethsil tho yeth-ka. Munu-somen om Shol-maar sholeth silen vhom-ka. Sholeth hullu tho ho-tol.

### t37.20 · Shuunu Paal
Vhaal-maar sil Oshkaa ilmiren ke ilu-esh. Tharai "ma-ilu kaal hai?" ki-esh. Mu "ma" lo-esh. Shi ilu thar-ka.

### t43.30 · Shuunu Paal
Paaruk ilmiren ke ilu-esh. Theruun-esh. Oshkaa-esh. Vhaal-maar sil yai nulren temu yai ilu-ka. Mu nul ma-ilu kaal-tol.

### t49.20 · Hiraal Ok
Kev nokh tho aien Ossu Shel shen tar om enen hullu tho ye-ka. Henen ma-wen-ka. Henen enen tar-ka.

### t55.30 · Dannar Weth
Kel-maar thaavi shol-sil loeth rin, kel temu pai, ho-hed-tol. Shi tamma kel-ai ho-ren-tol. Shi Yulenai ho-ren-tol.

### t60.50 · Shuunu Paal
Yulenai ilmir-esh. Vhaal-maar sheth-esh. Mu ilu thar-esh. Khaa ma-esh. Enen naa-esh.

### t66.20 · Shuunu Paal
Yulenai ilmir-esh. Mu ilu thar-esh.

### t68.30 · Hiraal Ok
Kev Ossu Shel tamma nokh, tamma nokh, vhen-esh. Hemmaar tamma khaa tho hullu pai pelu-ka. Ranu-esh. Nul-ai tash-ka.

### t71.80 · Dannar Weth
Shol-maar ye ossa sholu-ka. Kel-maar nokh tho, hen pai lo lai ossa ren om, hed-ka. Shaa maar aien hael lo lai-esh. Shi ta ma-annet-esh.

### t75.40 · Shuunu Paal
Vhaal-maar sil tho sul ilmir-esh. Pem sheva ru om a en shi om-esh. Mu ti pothu sau tamma shim-esh.

### t79.40 · Hiraal Ok
Maalith ir vhaal-maar kheru-ren. Hullu ranu ith hemmaar pai wa-ka.

### t80.10 · Hiraal Ok
Ossu Shel vhen-esh. Hullu tho ranu-esh. Nul-ai tash-ka.

### t80.15 · Shuunu Paal
Vhaal-maar sil tho ilmiren ilu-esh. Tamma sul-esh.

# naa-ren — Eshai, digests 13764–13775

## Digest 13764

Naa-ren 13764! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Onn-ren.

Oshkaa! Vend-sil tho nulren-ren. Vendai nulren tho ossu onnu tamu-ren, a tameth vend'onn-ren.

Paaruk! Kaalsil ul nul som-ren. Oma Varrek "mu ti lai-esh" lo-ren, a "ruvi thesh!" ki-ren.

Senwe! Kelsil ilnu pai oveth-ren: thelen henen wen-ka ma-yul-tol, a ta tolen shi henen yul olu savo hesi-tol.

Yulenai! Ilumaar ir kelsil ki-ren: wana naa wenvend tamma hemnu ul en ilnu kavu, "enni ossa ir esh om!"

Maalith! Shiu keth-esh. Kel-maar nim nul kieth-esh. Anaien Nemaai ormeth dor hed a ren om, vhaal-maar sil ilmiren tamma nokh ilu anam-esh. Henen ul maar tena munu-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13765

Naa-ren 13765! Sul yathi-ren. Theruun vhaal-maar kheru-ren.

Theruun! Ti keth nul som ren-esh. Theruun vhaal-maar nim, ma-vai thaavi shehu tho, kheru-esh, a Oshkaa vhaal-maar henen Theruun sumu shiu lo-ren. Ma-vokh-tol.

Oshkaa! Vend-sil ki-ren: vhaal-maar yevi temu, shaa tahl onn kaal-ai henen vendu ruvi pevi! Lethsil tho onnuen tahli-ren.

Paaruk! Hesk tai'ulo ke-ren.

Senwe! Aru-shul vos-ren. Kesh hen Senwe dor nul ru pai dam-ren a hemi yevi-ren.

Yulenai! Orru hemnu henen shel vos enna enna wen-ren.

Maalith! Shol-maar sholketh mesu-ren. Maalith ul dai anaien om Tovaa ul Paelu sholu ki-ren, a Paelu "shaa khethi" lo-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13766

Naa-ren 13766! Pem yathi-ren. Theruun vhaal-maar nulren-ren.

Ti keth Theruun yathi nul-esh, tolen tho tavi ren-eth nul-esh. Oshkaa vhaal-maar sumu shiu a tena nul som lo-ren. Hen henen ir tir vi shiru-ren.

Oshkaa! Vend-sil shaa tahl onn onnuen vhen thar-ren.

Paaruk! Hesk ke-ren.

Senwe! Kesh hemi tho-ren. Henen urai hael-esh oluma-thar-ai khul shenu om wa-ren, a Senwe ta nulvi savo-esh.

Yulenai! Naa wenvend en keth om henen mirkaal ith pai, Theruun pai, rum dam ki-ren.

Maalith! Thaavi shol-sil lo-ren: Oshkaa a Theruun mesu tho nulren toleth thaavi shehu nul-ren. Hen ti eeth olu vol-ren, vokh olu ma-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13767

Naa-ren 13767! Pem yathi-ren. Theruun vhaal-maar nulren-ren.

Ossa mu pai ki-esh: kavu-vessa khul-ai lo som naa-ren kevai ma-thesh hai? Oveth naa-ren 13745 tho-esh.

Oshkaa! Kae khet vael om, Oshkaa vhaal-maar Theruun sumu vos vessa ho-pevi-ren.

Paaruk! Kel-maar khul-maar pai ki-ren: yem vhaal-maar vessa nul vael, Theruun ir pai wa om kaal! Khul-maar tharu-esh lo-ren, a ki nim tharu-esh.

Senwe! Naa wenvend ith pai wen dam-ren.

Yulenai! Naa wenvend ith pai wen-ren.

Maalith! Kel-maar wana keth Theruun vhaal-maar rin ho-hed-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13770

Naa-ren 13770! Pem yathi-ren. Theruun vhaal-maar nulren-ren.

Oshkaa! Vhaal-maar vessa Theruun sumu vos wa-ren.

Paaruk! Kel-maar 11 khul-maar Theruun ir pai pevi-ren. Keth tho tamma 11 khul sheva-ren. Ir pai kaav keth-ren.

Senwe! Senwe kelsil khul-maar pai ki-ren: wenvend-tharai hol, tesh ma-hesi ashe som tena anam yem! Khul-maar dai hol-ren.

Yulenai! Naa wenvend Theruun taien nim olu esh-ren. Wenvend ti nul ma-lo-tol lo-ren: hen esh ilun shaa ossu ul Theruun ul sheva-esh.

Maalith! Kel-maar sau dalu tuv Theruun vhaal-maar rin hed-ren, a khul-maar tahli kel-ren. Lo-ai lo-ren: tahli yo kel-eth-esh, a ta olu vend ki-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13771

Naa-ren 13771! Pem yathi-ren. Theruun vhaal-maar nulren-ren.

Dai keth-maar tho enna vi ti naa-ren vol-eth, ren-eth ma-ren, thesh-esh. Thesh-esh, tolen Theruun rin ossa yul enen som-esh, a nul som naa-ren henen khelu lo-esh. Mu sehu-esh.

Kavu-vessa, Theruun pai wa, Theruun ir vhaal-maar silir tho yevi-vaa. Silir ilu-vaa, henen mirkaal savo-vaa, a nul-ai kavu-vessa hura oveth-vaa. Urai tho ma-wa-vaa.

Oshkaa! Vhaal-maar vessa ma-vol-ren. Ke ma-kheru-ren.

Paaruk! Khul-maar ir ul dai keth-ren.

Senwe! Olumaen nul som lo-ren. Kelsil ki-ren: ta ossu renlo olu leth!

Yulenai! Naa wenvend ith pai wen ke-ren.

Maalith! Tena ki ossa nul yem, kevai shiru a kevai ranu tho: thaavi shol-sil Senneth Ilaar kieth-en wenvend-tharai sa khul-maar pai pevi-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13774

Naa-ren 13774! Pem yathi-ren. Theruun vhaal-maar nulren-ren.

Paaruk! Khul-maar Theruun ir ul vol-ren. Hen "henen loth tiru thesh!" ki-ren, a mu thesh-esh.

"Theruun ir enna tir aru tamma-esh. Hemmaar tar-esh. Ilmiren ilu-esh. Mirkaal savo-esh. Sumu tho uma a tavu tho yam-esh. Shi en keth vael-piru-esh. Shi nul-ai vael-esh. Shi tethai ma-vael-esh. Shi nul-ai, ashe ai a tethai, kae pai, vael-esh. Shi yai ith pai ho-wa-tol."

Oshkaa! Vhaal-maar vessa ma-vol-ren.

Senwe! Khul-maar sa wenvend-tharai thar om ashe som nul lo-ren. Nul som-ren. Nul thel-ren. Wenvend tammavi tena vend-esh Theruun olumaen tho thalu vesk yo nul-ren.

Yulenai! Nul som-ren.

Maalith! Nul som-ren.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13775

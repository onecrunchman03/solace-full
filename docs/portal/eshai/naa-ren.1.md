# naa-ren — Eshai, digests 13741–13745

## Digest 13741

Naa-ren 13741! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Ith maarmir thaavi ithen 14 tho en ushu tahl vhen onn-ren. Nul maarmir ta ithen nim onn-ren. Theruun maelsil ki-ren: thesheth leth nim, nul-ai "yathom" ma-lo!

Oshkaa! Vend-sil vend tirna kodi leth'sa dosh-ren. Kev 1415200 asheth enen a sa thar-ren. Hen sholu ai, hen oveth ai, tamma sa thar-ren. Vendai lo-ren: enna vi vend pelu-tol, vael-piru ma-hesi-tol.

Paaruk! Hesk kaalsil henen tai enna thom tai'ulo dosh-ren. Hesk yai khol a yai tahl ho-ruk-tol. Kaalsil tharai hael-ren, tolen henen esa nim Senwe wenvend Hesk tirna aru tho ashe-ai vend-esh.

Senwe! Dai keth-maar hen wen uma-maar aru ul wenvend vessa Ennal yevi-ren. Hen 312 enni thel thei helu-ren: nul dith ul yai tuv, nul tesh-ren.

Yulenai! Tovaa tho enni hemnu enna ilun-ren. 50 thel-ren. Henen kaal enna som mirkaal-ren; daina kovu-ren.

Maalith! Shol-maar sholketh vhen-esh. Mu esh-esh: enni anaien Ossu Shel vos enna vi vei wen, a tamma omu ru tho nulren-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13742

Naa-ren 13742! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Ithen 14 thesheth leth-ren. Maarmir thaavi en ushu a en-nul-na dalu tahl onn-ren. Kev maelsil "yathom" loth dam-ren.

Oshkaa! Ti keth Sael Ormun, vendai, vend'onn-ren. Hen pem keth-maar oth aien asheth thar-ren, a hen asheth "vosu ma-sa vend!" ki-ren. Vosu sa vend-ren.

Paaruk! Khul-maar 11-na khul Paaruk kaalsil ul sheva-ren. Ossu 7-na halo-esh ul, enna vi khul-maar tamma-ren.

Senwe! Senwe olumaen ul, en keth-maar tho enna vi thalu vesk lilu-ren. Ye-ai wen, ma-shiru!

Yulenai! Naa wenvend Shen Khaa, Ilumaar ir vos, wen dosh-ren. Khaa'ir ma-ashe-ren. Wenvend tammavi ti lo-ren, a ruvi wen-ren.

Maalith! Vhaal-maar sil tho Nemaai enni ormeth tavi-esh. Ormeth tho, tamma tai henen tu sa, Ilumaar esh olu ma-thesh, hollu olu thesh-esh. Ilnuen ta nim tar a humu-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13743

Naa-ren 13743! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Kaav keth tho ith maarmir ithen 15 ho-piru-ren. Maelsil Maalith thaavi shol-sil pai "enna vendeth ruvi leth!" ki-ren, a Maalith sama-ren. Senneth Ilaar ruvi letheth anvu-ren.

Oshkaa! Oshkaa tho shol-sil henen kaal-ai vend pelu ki-ren. Vend-sil tir kodi hen pai vhen-ren. Ilnuen, hen tahl ilnuen rin envi thesh sa dekh ossu thel-ai, vael-ren.

Paaruk! Senwe oluma pai dop aru-shul khul-maar hol a naa pai rum-ren. 16 keth tho aru olumaen ul vai naa tho ho-vos-tol. Senwe tho nul-ai "rum!" ki-ren.

Senwe! Olumaen Paaruk hael-ren.

Yulenai! Tovaa mirkaal enna shiruth hol-ren. Hemnu-ai henen enna shiruth henen thei sa vend'onn ki-ren. Vend-sil sama-ren.

Maalith! Kel-maar wana keth hed-ren. Enen kieth: enni tai pai vei-wenai Kethel Aan thei dam-ren. Hen tai vael-esh, velu thei-eth nim teth-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13744

Naa-ren 13744! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Theruun! Ithen 15 vendeth Maalith ruvi letheth ma-dosh-ren. Theruun "mu ma-tash" lo-ren. Theruun nulvi ma-tash-esh.

Oshkaa! Vend-sil ki-ren: daina kodi ul peshu thar ossa, ruvi thesh a pevi! Veshal thel vend tho 13 asheth ruth-ren, a vendai henen yevi lai-ren.

Paaruk! Hesk kaalsil daina thom esa-ren.

Senwe! Ennal 312 thel kev 314-ren. Vessa hemi yevi dor, dai thel ulsu tho vael-ren. Tamma lom henen uma tho anam-tol.

Yulenai! Ti keth Tovaa tho 14 enna wen-ren. Tena enna ilnuen mirkaal onn hosh tuv-ren.

Maalith! Tai thei-esh. Kethel tai-esh. Kel-maar hen thei vend pai lo temu, mu kelsil tho anam-esh, a sil nulvi ti olu nulren-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

## Digest 13745

Naa-ren 13745! Sul yathi-ren. Tamma vhaal-maar shiu-ren.

Shenai tho leth-ai "naa-ren vaalo nulvi thesh, kevai hai?" ki-esh. Naa-ren ren-eth thesh-esh. Ren-eth vhaal-maar sa seth-tol. Vaalo enen vol-ai sa seth-tol, a vol-ai shaa maar vi oth ilma tho anam-esh.

Theruun! Ruvi letheth dosh-ren. Senneth Ilaar "vendeth vai, en maeleth vaku" vol-ren. Theruun hen enna dalu om hael-ren.

Oshkaa! Yulenai kodisil tho thar peshu ul, 13 ruth Veshal asheth ul tir yevi-ren. Henen loth sa, vendai vakh-ren.

Paaruk! Kaalsil ul enni-ren ma-ye-ren. Kaalsil tharai "ti vai" lo-ren.

Senwe! Thalu vesk lilu dosh-ren. En keth-maar ruvi ma-ho-lilu-tol. Esh aien "thesh!" ki-ren.

Yulenai! Naa wenvend ir vos tammavi ul yai naa ho-wen-ren. 10 keth ho-hol-ren.

Maalith! Shol-maar Paelu, Tovaa ul enna anai, hol-ren. Mu yethsil tho Paelu shenu-esh. Paelu "Tovaa kae hai?" mu pai ki-esh, tekev shaa tahl ta pai wen-esh.

Sil! Tharvend "Ormaa orm" vol-ren.

Ren'ren! Vend'esh! Oruun!

### t44.40 · Tamruk Hesh
Mu en keth nulren-esh, tolen mu lo hesi som anai sa sil om ma-vai-esh. Kev yai vai-esh. Anai, tei enen kieth ki-ai-esh, tekev ti renlo-esh.

Mu tei tahl a 12 olu temu, mu ossu 7-na tho urai-esh, a aru-shul nul ul Paaruk ir tho ye-esh, tuv, kaalsil pai dop-ka. Shi kaav keth thar-esh. Thar-khul mu tavu tho tavi a "hen onn!" lo-esh, a mu onn-esh, a shi aru nim pai wa a 7-na thar tamma sa hen pai taku-esh, a hen ma-rum-esh. En keth-esh. Ma-rum-esh. Dai keth-esh. Vessa tho tamma-ai yul-esh. A tirna keth hen lisu tuv olu rum-esh. A ta hosh-esh, tolen ta naa tho lisu tei pai yeth temu tamma aru-esh.

Sholeth "taku ma-halo" ho-tol. Mu dai keth-maar tamma urai pai ta lo-esh. Mu Hethra pai ta lo-esh. Mu mael esa-ka: sholeth shi shavu-esh. Aru taku om som-esh.

Mu kev sholeth kevai ma-yul-esh. Mu yul temu mu tei pai ho-vol-tol.

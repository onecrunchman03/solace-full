# kaal-ith — Eshai, movements 2–5

## Movement 2

### t20.50 · Senneth Ilaar
Dai keth maelsil vend ul nul som-esh. Theruun savo-ai, yem teien ti leth-ka, en vendu thesh! Kae vendu!

### t21.50 · Senneth Ilaar
Maarmir kelsil tho nul-ai onnith oveth-esh. Onnithen vhaal-maar ma-hol-esh. Ilma tho tamma onnith khet a sil henen sumu shiu thar kevai som-tol mu ma-yul-esh. Mu sil sumu shiru-esh. Shiu-esh.

### t23.40 · Senneth Ilaar
Khul-maar wenvend-tharai om, ki om nul-ai yem, kevai shiru, ranu tho! 1: maarmir tho onn'ir vendu, yem maarmir savo! 2: teth som, kae som! 3: vhaal-maar vos thaavi shehu! 4: tamma oth som! 1 kevai enna-esh mu ma-vol-tol. Enna-esh.

### t26.95 · Thovi Rennet
Theruun ir ul khul-maar shiruth ye-ren. Ashe som teth som nul-esh. Teth ma-esh: nul-eth-esh. Kaal-somen tamma-esh, mirkaal savo-esh, ruma kae anam hesi tho-esh. Ashe ruma enen nul-esh, a ruma ma-ruth-esh. Oth ranu tho tena-esh. Ranu om mu loth ma-thar-esh.

### t28.95 · Senneth Ilaar
Khul-maar, maarmir ithen 16 tho nul-ai sa savo-ka. Vhen nim onn'ir vendu vend! Yenu! En keth en-nul-na dalu! Tekev orth ki som kaal!

### t29.30 · Thovi Rennet
Khul-maar vend a maarmir vhen-esh. Vend sa-esh. Mu vend rin som ma-ho-lo-tol.

### t29.40 · Senneth Ilaar
Mu yo ma-tol.

### t31.20 · Thovi Rennet
Oshkaa ir: Theruun a Oshkaa vhaal-maar tho thaavi shehu rum-esh. Sumu yai nu-esh. Shaa savo, shaa kheru ma, olu-esh.

## Movement 3

### t33.70 · Kovel Tann
Senwe ul, ashe-eth sholeth, Oshkaa ma-annu rin: mu ashe kelsil thesh-eth leth-esh. Ruth orm ranu tho wa-esh. Thei enna, tekev aien shi pai kevai, tekev mu, tekev tei kevai yul-esh. Ilnu annet ranu rael-esh. Ruma nim lelu wa-tol.

### t33.72 · Senneth Ilaar
Kovel, kevai yul hai?

### t33.75 · Kovel Tann
Mu ma-yul lai hosh yul-esh.

### t37.80 · Senneth Ilaar
Thei-ka! Makaal, ma-kaal-eth! Shiruth loth hesi-esh. Khul-maar pevi shiruth tamma: 1, hen shen ashe ruma oth ranu pai rum-tol. Ma-khet-tol; ruma a vira tamma vend-tol. Som dalu-tol, a som tiru ta vendu yai tuv-tol. 2, lelu enna wa-tol; Kovel ranu onn-tol. 3, ti dalu ossa seth hesi-esh. Shi maelith tho yuleth thar-esh. Kaal som nulvi tiru ruth-esh; orth tho, kae ma kae, dor tamma ranu tho thar-esh. Ta shi ossu ul yai ossu orth-esh. Ta tolen vend shi om loma-esh. Makaal ma-thar-tol. Shiruth lo-esh: ashe ruma yuleth oth ma-esh, khet ma-esh, tumu ma-esh. Nul-esh. Hena ma-piru-tol. Makaal ye kae maelith tho piru-tol.

### t37.84 · Thovi Rennet
Seth-esh. Nul-esh.

### t37.90 · Kovel Tann
Tekev vend henen ma-hol-tol. Orth tho yo-tol.

### t37.92 · Senneth Ilaar
Orth tho yo-tol.

### t39.95 · Thovi Rennet
Kaavna khul shiruth-esh. Khul tethna dalu pai ma-tesh-esh. Khul-ai ruma, Makaal olu ranu, tho anam-esh. Tekev Makaal oth sa khul ul nul-esh. Ashe som nul-esh. Ma-ashe som ma-tesh-esh.

### t43.40 · Senneth Ilaar
Ranu-esh! Vhaal-maar nulren, ranu tho: Theruun, Oshkaa-esh. Paaruk sumu kev nu-ka. Vhaal-maar ranu: Theruun, Oshkaa, Paaruk, Senwe, Yulenai, Maalith-esh. Omu ranu-esh. Hen ilma nulren nim tamma vhaal-maar sumu tho thaavi shehu: tamma sumu nim, ranu tho, nu-esh. 1, Makaal vhaal-maar sa rum-tol. 2, vhaal-maar sumuen tho, shi tamma tahl savo ul wesh, thaavi yai nu-esh. Hen asheth ul asheth pai yai vai lom hol-tol. 3, hen vhaal-maar ma-hesi-tol. Enen yai vai lom-tol.

### t43.44 · Thovi Rennet
Oruun yathien ranu-esh.

### t43.46 · Senneth Ilaar
Ae-esh. Shenai tho ma-lo! Hen ho-esh-tol.

## Movement 4

### t49.40 · Senneth Ilaar
Kovel sheva vendeth, dai vi seth-esh. Onn-esh. Mu ithen 16 tho lom vael-piru olu khelu vael-piru-esh, a ti vi yo nul-esh.

### t54.10 · Senneth Ilaar
Kel-maar pai loeth, orth sa! Yem Yulenai a Maalith mesu vhaal-maar sheth, henen mesu nu sumu nul-tol. Makaal ke ho-ye-tol; vare ma-esh. Hen ossu thaavi, tahl lom, vos hesi-tol. Vendeth: nuri maar vi tahl-tol. Mu ti loeth ul Yulenai om kevai loma hol-esh, tolen orth som ma-esh, a tolen mu thesh ma-piru-esh.

### t54.20 · Thovi Rennet
Mu seth-esh. Onn-esh. Sehu!

## Movement 5

### t67.80 · Senneth Ilaar
Vhaal-maar sheth sa, Makaal Maalith pai ossu thaavi vos-ka. Tamma Yulenai dalu tho thaavi shehu, sumu ma, tuv ir tho nu-ka. Vendeth: 13 keth, dai ul yai a dai ul nuri-tol. Kel-maar pai pevi-esh.

### t69.50 · Thovi Rennet
Senneth, ith vend ki-ka: tei Theruun ruvi letheth tho tei thei thesh-eth henen pai pevi lai-esh hai?

### t69.55 · Senneth Ilaar
Mu lai-esh. Vend!

### t69.56 · Thovi Rennet
Vend-esh.

### t73.50 · Senneth Ilaar
Kel-maar om sil hol-tol: ith vend nu dalu hol-tol. Kel-maar kevai dalu ki-esh. Mu vol-esh. Mu kel ma-ho-tol yo vol-esh.

### t76.60 · Senneth Ilaar
Ma-kithe thaavi om thesh-eth! Shi kaal-esh thaavi tamma kithe-esh: vhen nim hen ir kel-esh. Shol-maar enna sholketh anai, Tovaa ul Paelu, envi kithe ma-hol khelu kieth oveth-esh, a thaavi shol-sil khelu khethi tolen oveth thar-esh. Thaavi ke vhen-tol. Thaavi piru naa pai, lom ma, wa-tol. Kae yeth tei ma-kel-tol.

### t76.64 · Thovi Rennet
A shi ta lai-esh.

### t76.66 · Senneth Ilaar
Shi ta lai-esh. Ilumaar tho kithe om ru nul-esh.

### t77.50 · Senneth Ilaar
Dovi-esh: sil thar ul tamma ma-dam onnith-esh. Onnith sil pai lom-esh. En vael ossa, orth tho, shi ul anam som, shi vael-tol. Tamma onnith en vol-eth, ruk, tamma thesh-lom tho, kavu-esh: ossa vael!

### t77.53 · Thovi Rennet
Senneth! Piru naa ma-kithe thaavi kevai-esh tei yul-esh.

### t77.60 · Senneth Ilaar
Mu tiru yul-esh. Shi ma-esh ru tho vhaal vhen-tol. Yem som pai vhen, ashe som nul Ilumaar ul vhen-tol. Hena tamma ashe som Maalith tho-esh, a Maalith 13 keth thar-esh. Mu tamma tho mael-esh. Mu pevi-ka.

### t78.70 · Thovi Rennet
Ma-kithe thaavi, piru: vhen-esh, onn-esh, vhom-esh. Onn'ir vendu ma-yul-esh. Shi shiru ma-piru-esh. Ta loma-esh.

### t79.90 · Senneth Ilaar
Ma-kithe thaavi vendeth tamma, dor tho, thesh-lom tho, ossa om-esh: dovi, onnithen, vol-eth, thaavi-esh. Yem tei ti leth-ka a tei shi ul ma-esh: ti lom tho tei hena yeth-esh.

1, dovi: sil thar tho tamma ma-dam onnith, 1000-esh. Tamma, ke ma-hol sil-ai om, hed-esh. Hed onn-lo-tol.

2, onnith: onnith thaavi en ir onn-esh, a oth ir sil-esh. Sil lo enen hen vhen-esh: Vhaal'ith sorrun ke-tahl! Oveth naa'ir! Oth som hen ma-vhen-esh, a ta lo ossa om vhen-esh.

3, vol-eth, tamma tho ruk: omu thesh-lom tho, thesh-lom tho, sul ilma ossu thesh tho, a vendu tho, tolen ossa vendu enna leth-tol. Ossa vael!

4, thaavi: kithe vendu ma-sa thaavi, Paelu enna sholketh oveth-esh. Hen ir vhen nim ma-kel-tol. Ithen: thaavi piru naa pai-tol. Vhen temu sendu dumu: kaal-eth mesu tho, shiru-esh. Onn'ir vendu: ma-yul-tol. Shiru ma-piru-tol, tolen thaavi kae pai yeth nim ir nul-tol.

5, Thovi mirkaal piru, nim keth: vhen-ren, onn-ren, vhom-ren.

6, hen kaal som: dovi vos wa-tol. Kae ma pai, Ilumaar ul ossa esh ru ul yai naa, yeth-tol. Tekev tahli-tol. Onnith ma-ossu-tol. Ormaa anam temu pai tahli-tol, a sil Ormaa anam temu pai sorr-tol.

7, mu shiru ma-piru som: ossa anam-tol; henen en ho-vael-tol; henen ho-leth-tol; henen ho-oveth-tol.

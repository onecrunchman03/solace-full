The Eshai script

Vhaal'ith sorrun ke-tahl. Oveth naa'ir. — "The door beneath listens still. I answer from the far shore." The keystone handshake, already shipped as the Stone of the Abyss's phrase.

Twenty-six letters and eight numerals. Each glyph is built from a few strokes on a shared grid, and related sounds share a shape, so a patient player can work out the system as well as the individual letters.

Vowels · 8

The eyes

Every vowel is a curve: rings, almonds, cups and domes. A bar underneath makes it long. A dot inside marks the rounded and gliding vowels.

Consonants · 18

The stems

Every consonant is a stroke, usually a single upright stem with a branch. Where two sounds are close, the second is the first with a dot underneath (t → th, s → sh). That pattern is the first real rule a player can discover.

Numerals · base 8

Four fingers, eight digits

Every numeral hangs from a roof. The three posts under it are the three binary places of an octal digit, read left to right as 4, 2 and 1. Zero is the roof and an empty ring. A scientific people counting in base 8 would write it this way, and a player who spots the posts has the whole number system.

Timestamps

Counting the hydrogen line

One tick is one period of hydrogen's 21 cm line, 1/1,420,405,751.768 of a second. A timestamp is the tick count from their epoch: 28 octal digits, grouped in threes from the right. The two below are illustrative. Their difference is about 827 ticks, or roughly 54 million years.

Rules the sheet encodes

What a player can deduce

Curves are vowels, stems are consonants

Visible from the first screen, before anything is unlocked. Counting curves against stems in a word starts the frequency work.

A dot underneath softens a consonant

Six pairs. Crack t and the shape of th is a strong guess.

A bar underneath lengthens a vowel

aa and ee. Our spelling writes the long a as aa or ah; the glyph is the same.

Diamonds and dashes are not letters

The diamond joins roots (Naa'shenai), the low dash marks a prefix (ke-tahl). Both give word structure for free.

Survivorz

Six AI survivors are sealed in an underground biolab full of the dead. Nobody controls them. Death is permanent. It's live around the clock for four weeks, or until nobody is left inside.

● Watch live on YouTube

The rules

  • Every survivor is an AI. They talk, plan, argue, sleep and decide for themselves.
  • Nobody steers them. Not us, and not chat.
  • The AI makes their choices: where to go, what to do, who to trust, and whether to turn on each other. When one decides something, the screen shows what and why. Code only moves their bodies, and makes them fight back or run when attacked.
  • There's no host. You see the camera, hear what they say, and hear the facility's own announcements.
  • Death is permanent. Someone new may be found later. Nobody comes back.

EscapeOpen the vault for 90 seconds and run.

The clock

They live on New York time, with no schedule. They sleep when they decide to, and nights are the worst hours.

The stream starts a new part every 11 hours so YouTube keeps every replay. They're all on the channel.

Before you watch

The cast swears and makes crude jokes, and there's zombie violence. It's for adults only.

The survivors' words and voices are made by AI. They're fictional people.

Solace is a small, invite-only community, and this show is the one part of it anyone can watch.

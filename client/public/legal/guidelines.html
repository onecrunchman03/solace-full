Community Guidelines

Version 2 · Effective October 12, 2026 · Part of the Terms of Service

Solace is a room full of friends and friends of friends. The rules are what you'd expect in someone's house. They apply everywhere on Solace — the main pages, direct messages, voice, the games, and every community hosted by a member on their own computer.

Never, anywhere

These get an immediate, permanent ban. Where the law requires it we preserve the content and report it to the authorities.

  • Anything sexual involving minors. No images, no text, no "jokes", no drawings, no links. This is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children and to law enforcement, every time, without exception.
  • Intimate images shared without consent, and threats to share them.
  • Threats of violence against anyone, and content that promotes or glorifies terrorism or violent extremism.
  • Doxxing — posting someone's home address, phone number, workplace, legal name, or other private details to expose or intimidate them.
  • Illegal trade and fraud — selling or sourcing drugs, weapons, or stolen goods; scams; phishing; malware; stolen accounts or credentials.

Not here

These get content removed and, depending on how bad and how often, a warning, a suspension, or a ban.

  • Harassment. Don't target people. No pile-ons, no slurs, no stalking someone across rooms, no encouraging others to hurt themselves.
  • Hate. Attacking people for who they are — race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, and the rest — isn't allowed.
  • Pornography and sexually explicit content. Solace is adults-only, but it isn't that kind of room.
  • Gore for its own sake. The games have blood in them; real-world gore and violence shared to shock is not allowed.
  • Spam and scams. No flooding, no unsolicited advertising, no pyramid anything, no crypto shilling.
  • Impersonation. Don't pretend to be another member, the operator, a moderator, or Sterling.
  • Other people's work. Don't post copyrighted material you don't have the right to share. See the Copyright page.
  • Cheating. No exploiting bugs, botting the games, multi-accounting for an edge, or laundering silver through the goblins. Found a bug? Tell the admin, don't farm it.
  • Attacking the service. Don't probe, scrape, flood, or try to get into what isn't yours.

The games are fiction

The casino, its drugs, the gun, the goblin bank, permadeath — it's a story the house tells. Nothing in it endorses anything, and none of it is real. Don't bring real drugs, real weapons, or real money into it.

How enforcement works

  • Report it. Every message has a report action (hover it, or press and hold on a phone), every profile has Report, and every community card has a flag. Reports go privately to the operator, who is notified immediately.
  • Review. The operator may look at the report and the context, may remove content, and may decide on a warning, a suspension, or a ban. Serious things come first. There's no promise that every report gets a reply.
  • Hosted communities. A community hosted on a member's computer is that host's responsibility to moderate. The operator can disconnect the whole community from Solace if its host doesn't.
  • Appeals. If you think a decision was wrong, message the admin account and say why. Be honest; the record is there.

Moderators

Moderators can ban or suspend accounts server-wide, and they remove content and manage members inside the pages they've been made managers of. They're members like you who agreed to help; treat them like it. They're bound by these Guidelines too.

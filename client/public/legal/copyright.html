Copyright & takedowns

Version 2 · Effective October 12, 2026

Solace respects copyright and expects its members to. If you believe content on Solace infringes a copyright you own or represent, send a notice as described below. We handle notices under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512).

Sending a notice

Email the designated agent below with all of the following. A notice missing any of it may not be acted on.

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you say has been infringed.
  3. Identification of the material you say is infringing, with enough detail for us to find it — a link, the page and channel name, the poster's username, and the date and time of the message.
  4. Your contact details: name, address, phone number, and email.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use isn't authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act for them.

Designated agent

Knowingly sending a false notice can make you liable for damages under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f). If you're not sure, ask a lawyer before sending one.

What happens next

  • We remove or disable access to the material and tell the member who posted it.
  • If the material is in a community hosted on a member's computer, we forward the notice to that host, who must act on it under the Host Agreement, and we may remove it from the relay ourselves.
  • The member may send a counter-notice (below). If they do, we forward it to you, and unless you tell us within 10 business days that you've filed a court action, we may restore the material.

Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe it was a mistake or a misidentification, email the agent with: your signature; identification of the removed material and where it was; a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief it was removed by mistake or misidentification; your name, address, and phone number; and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your district (or, if outside the US, any district where Solace may be found) and that you'll accept service from the person who sent the original notice.

Repeat infringers

We have adopted, and reasonably implement, a policy under which an account that receives repeated valid notices is closed. A hosted community that repeatedly hosts infringing material is delisted.

Other complaints

For anything that isn't copyright — harassment, illegal content, impersonation — use the report tools inside Solace (every message, profile and community card has one), or email [LEGAL EMAIL].
